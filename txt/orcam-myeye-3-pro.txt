Detailed Description:
The OrCam MyEye 3 Pro is a single slim bar about the size of a finger: 3 inches long, about 0.8 inch high and 0.6 inch thick, weighing 22.5 grams (under an ounce). It clips magnetically to the side of a pair of glasses, with no cable or separate box. The camera, speaker, battery and controls are all inside the bar.
The 13-megapixel camera is at the front end, facing forward, with built-in lights to illuminate text. A small built-in speaker reads aloud near your ear; OrCam Bluetooth earphones are an option for privacy.
The outer long side carries a raised touch bar. Tap it to start or stop reading, double-tap to pause or resume (or, when idle, to open the setup menu), and swipe forward or back to change the volume or skip through text. Holding a finger on the bar while facing a person about 3 feet away teaches it a face; it can store up to 100. You can also point a finger at text, or say "Hey OrCam" followed by a command.
The inner long side, against the glasses, has two magnets you can feel with a fingertip, and the power button next to them. Hold power for 2 seconds to turn it on; press it until you hear "Suspended, press again to shut down" to turn it off. It charges from a standard 5-volt phone charger through USB-C.
In the box (from UK dealer VisionAid): the MyEye 3 Pro, a stand, a speaker, a neck lanyard, unisex glasses, three magnetic mounts, a USB power adapter and cable, and a case. Placed on the stand and connected by USB-C or Wi-Fi to a computer browser at cast.orcam.com, it works as a desktop magnifier with an AI assistant that answers questions about a scanned page.

History:
OrCam released the MyEye 3 Pro in 2024 as the current generation of its clip-on MyEye reader. It is a single bar that clips magnetically to glasses with no cable. It adds a desktop mode: on its stand, it connects to a browser at cast.orcam.com and has an AI assistant that answers questions about a scanned page.
