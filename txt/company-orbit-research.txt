Orbit Research
Type: Company profile
Founded: 1998
Products listed: 7

History:
Founded by Venkatesh Chari. Makes low-cost braille: the Orbit Reader 20 (for APH, 2017), Orbit Reader 40, the Graphiti tactile graphics display (2016), the Orbit Speak (2022), the Slate multiline displays (2022) and the Orbit Reader Q40 (2025).

Graphiti (Braille Displays, Announced August 2, 2016 (APH and Orbit Research))
Orbit Reader 20 (Braille Displays, Announced March 2016, shipped 2017)
Orbit Reader 40 (Braille Displays, Announced March 11, 2020 (CSUN))
Orbit Slate 340 / 520 (Braille Displays, 2022)
Orbit Speak (Notetakers, Announced at CSUN, March 2022)
Orbit Reader Q40 (Braille Displays, 2025)
