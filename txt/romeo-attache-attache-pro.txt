Detailed Description:
The Romeo Attaché is a small, portable, single-sided impact braille embosser for teachers and students. It measures 17 × 11.25 × 5.75 inches and weighs 16.75 pounds.
It embosses at 15 characters per second on a line of up to 32 characters.
It uses tractor-feed paper up to 8.5 inches wide, letter-size, with sprocket holes along the edges.
It supports 6- and 8-dot braille and graphics.
Its launch price wasn't found; Enabling Technologies' price list effective July 1, 2012 shows the Romeo Attaché at $2,295 and the Attaché Pro at $2,495.

History:
Enabling Technologies of Jensen Beach, Florida made the Romeo Attaché and the Attaché Pro as its smallest embossers. They share one user's manual with the Romeo 25 and Romeo Pro 50, and the November 2010 revision describes them. Both are single-sided impact embossers that print 15 characters per second and up to 32 cells per line on sprocket-fed paper 1.5 to 8.5 inches wide. Each weighs 16.75 pounds in a desktop case with a handle. The Pro adds ET Speaks voice feedback, which announces keypad entries and status, and a single-sheet mode. Every model plays an excerpt from Tchaikovsky's Romeo and Juliet when its power-on self-test passes.
