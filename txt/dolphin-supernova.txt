History:
Dolphin launched SuperNova in 1998 as, in its words, the first fully integrated magnifier and screen reader, developed at its Worcester headquarters. SuperNova USB followed in 2005 as the first assistive technology to run portably from a USB thumb drive. The Dolphin Pen could carry SuperNova, Hal or Lunar from PC to PC, and was nominated for a British Chamber of Commerce innovation award in 2005. SuperNova remains one of Dolphin's flagship products.
Predecessors: Hal for Windows (Dolphin Computer Access, By 1997 (Hal 1.04 for Windows 95))
