Victor Reader Stream firmware
Company: HumanWare
Category: Operating system or software version history
Versions listed: 5

Overview:
Firmware for HumanWare's Stream players. Stream 3 updates install over Wi-Fi or from an SD card.

Version History:

Version 1: Stream (1st generation) version 3
Released: 2009
Notes: Shown at CSUN 2009.

Version 2: Stream 3 v1.4
Released: October 2024
Notes: User guide revision.

Version 3: Stream 3 v1.5
Released: June 11, 2025
Notes: Rename notes on the device, bookmark alerts restored, Bookshare API 2.0, switching between text and audio in dual-format books, moving all podcasts to SD, and many bug fixes.

Version 4: Stream 3 v1.6
Released: February 2026
Notes: Experimental eBraille support (back-translated and read aloud).

Version 5: Stream 3 v1.7
Released: September 23, 2026
Notes: eSpeak NG alongside Acapela (up to 4 voices), NLS BARD 2, skippable DAISY 2 elements, larger text files, looping and play-all highlight bookmarks, bookshelf search, and Bluetooth device battery reporting.

Devices and Software That Ran It:
Victor Reader Stream (1st generation) (HumanWare, Announced July 2007 and shipping from early September 2007 (AccessWorld). The second-generation Stream replaced it in February 2013.)
Victor Reader Stream (2nd generation) (HumanWare, February 2013)
Victor Reader Stream (3rd generation) (HumanWare, 2023 to present)
Victor Reader Trek (HumanWare, 2017)

System Requirements:
Not documented
