Detailed Description:
The Solo V2 from BioSense Medical Devices was the cheapest fully talking meter in AccessWorld's 2012 review, at $17. It measures 3.92 by 2.16 by 0.71 inches and weighs 2.2 ounces.
Below its 1.5 by 1.9 inch display are two joined buttons: Power and Memory on the left, Repeat on the right. Their icons are not tactile, and AccessWorld found them hard to tell apart. The speaker is beneath them.
On the top of the right side are up and down buttons with raised arrows and, below them, a small round Settings button. These need considerable force to press. A mini-USB data port is at the bottom of the right side. There is no headphone jack.
It holds 500 readings, more than the other meters tested, and has an alarm setting. It does not speak the time of past readings, though the screen shows it.
For people with low vision, its 1-inch characters were the largest of any meter tested, with a 73.9% contrast ratio.
