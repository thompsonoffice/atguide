Detailed Description:
The BrailleSense 7, unveiled at CSUN 2026, comes in 20-, 32- and 40-cell models. The 32-cell model measures 9.84 × 5.53 × 0.89 inches and weighs under 1.54 pounds; the 40-cell is 11.86 inches wide and under 1.80 pounds.
It keeps the Perkins braille keyboard and adds a detachable QWERTY keyboard that attaches magnetically at the top through a secondary battery module. It also has a physical dictation button and an analog scroll wheel.
Its braille display has no physical routing buttons. Active Responsive Touch tracks your finger and refreshes the line automatically when you reach the last cell. To route the cursor you double-tap the display or press a front end button while touching the spot.
It has a visual LCD, HDMI output, dual USB-C and dual USB-A ports, a micro SD slot, a 4-pole headphone jack, stereo speakers and microphones, a haptic motor, dual-band GPS and a 20-megapixel rear camera with flash.
Inside are an octa-core processor up to 3.35 GHz, 8 GB of LPDDR5X RAM and 256 GB of storage, running Android 15. A long press on Home summons Google Gemini. The 4,600 mAh battery is removable and charges at 18 W through a magnetic connector.
