History:
Hal for DOS was one of the first products of Dolphin Computer Access, founded in 1986 in Worcester, UK and led by Noel Duffy. It was a software screen reader that spoke through a hardware synthesizer, Dolphin's Apollo. Dolphin says the pairing was sold in more than 30 languages and became an industry leader worldwide.
Successors: Hal for Windows (Dolphin Computer Access, By 1997 (Hal 1.04 for Windows 95))
