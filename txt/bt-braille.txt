History:
Blazie Technologies launched the BT Speak in 2024 as a modern, Linux-based revival of the Braille 'n Speak. In March 2026 it demonstrated the BT Braille, which the NFB's 2026 timeline describes as a modern take on the Braille Lite, the 1993 Blazie Engineering notetaker that added a refreshable braille display to the Braille 'n Speak.
Predecessors: BT Speak / BT Speak Pro (Blazie Technologies, 2024 to present)
