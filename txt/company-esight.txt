eSight
Type: Company profile
Founded: 2006
Products listed: 5

History:
Founded by Conrad Lewis to build electronic glasses for people with low vision. Models: eSight 1 (2013), eSight 2 (2015), eSight 3 (2017), eSight 4 (2020) and eSight Go (2024). Gentex announced its acquisition in January 2024.

Products:
eSight (1st generation) (Vision Wearables, 2013)
eSight 2 (Vision Wearables, May 2015)
eSight 3 (Vision Wearables, February 2017)
eSight 4 (Vision Wearables, 2020)
eSight Go (Vision Wearables, Announced January 3, 2023 (CES); commercial availability 2024)
