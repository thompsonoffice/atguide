Type Lite
Company: Blazie Engineering
Category: Notetakers
Released: By March 2001 (date of user guide)

Overview:
A notetaker from the Braille Lite family with a laptop-style Windows keyboard, a 40-cell braille display and an internal speech synthesizer.

Specs:
Released: By March 2001 (date of user guide)
Processor: Not documented
Memory (RAM): 2 MB
Storage: 12 MB flash plus CompactFlash
Operating system: Not documented
Default speech synthesizer: Internal synthesizer (model not documented)
Battery: Not documented
Display or braille cells: 40 cells
Price at launch: Not documented

Physical Description:
Laptop-style Windows keyboard behind a 40-cell braille display, with cursor-routing buttons and two advance bars.

Features:
Laptop-style QWERTY keyboard
40-cell display with routing buttons
Two advance bars
Internal speech synthesizer
Date book, phone book and calculator

History:
Documented in a Freedom Scientific user guide (part 440001-001) dated March 2001.

Ports:
Round 8-pin serial port on the back; CompactFlash slot
