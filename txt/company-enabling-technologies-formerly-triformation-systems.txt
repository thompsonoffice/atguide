Enabling Technologies (formerly Triformation Systems)
Type: Company profile
Founded: Not documented
Products listed: 5

History:
A US embosser maker. Under the Triformation name it built the BD-3 (1971) and LED-120 (1974). Later embossers include the Juliet (1992), the Romeo Attaché (2004) and the Juliet 120.

Products:
Triformation BD-3 (Embossers, 1971)
Triformation LED-120 (Embossers, 1974)
Enabling Technologies Juliet (Embossers, 1992)
Romeo Attaché / Attaché Pro (Embossers, 2004)
Juliet 120 (Embossers, Not documented)
