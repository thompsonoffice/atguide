Enabling Technologies (formerly Triformation Systems)
Type: Company profile
Founded: Triformation Systems incorporated in Florida on January 14, 1974
Products listed: 7

History:
A US embosser maker. Under the Triformation name it built the BD-3 (1971) and LED-120 (1974). Later embossers include the Juliet (1992), the Romeo Attaché (2004) and the Juliet 120.

Products:
Triformation BD-3 (Embossers, 1971)
Triformation LED-120 (Embossers, 1974)
Enabling Technologies Juliet (Embossers, 1992)
Juliet 120 (Embossers, Introduction year not found. The Juliet 120 is a later model developed jointly with Index Braille, so the original Juliet's 1992 date does not apply to it.)
Romeo Attaché / Attaché Pro (Embossers, 2004)
Romeo Pro 50 (Embossers, Sold alongside the Romeo 25 in Enabling Technologies' brochure. The introduction year was not found in that brochure or at atkratter.com.)
Romeo RB-25 (Romeo 25) (Embossers, In use by 2006, per an Enabling Technologies brochure. The introduction year was not found.)
