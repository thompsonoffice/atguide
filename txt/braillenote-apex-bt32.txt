BrailleNote Apex BT32
Company: HumanWare
Category: Notetakers
Released: November 2009

Overview:
A thinner, lighter BrailleNote running Windows CE 6.

Released: November 2009
Processor: Freescale i.MX31
Memory (RAM): 256 MB SDRAM
Storage: 8 GB flash
Operating system: Windows CE 6 with KeySoft

Battery: Not documented
Display or braille cells: 32 cells
Speech synthesizers available: Not documented
