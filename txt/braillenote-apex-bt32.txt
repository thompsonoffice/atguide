Detailed Description:
The BrailleNote Apex BT32 is a thin, light notetaker that HumanWare called the thinnest and lightest braille notetaker anywhere. A dealer lists it at about 9.6 × 5.6 × 0.78 inches and 1.8 pounds (812 g). It ships in a slim wraparound leather case with a carrying strap, and you can use it without taking it out of the case.
On top is an eight-key braille keyboard like a braillewriter: six dot keys, plus Backspace (dot 7) under the left little finger and Enter (dot 8) under the right. A small scroll wheel sits in the middle between the dot 1 and dot 4 keys, with the space bar just below it.
Nearest you is a 32-cell display of eight-dot braille cells, with a cursor routing button above each cell.
On the front edge facing you are four thumb keys, named Previous, Back, Advance and Next from left to right. They can be set for right- or left-handed use, so you can move through text without lifting your fingers from the braille.
Around the edges are three USB ports, an SD card slot, earphone and microphone jacks, an Ethernet port and a VGA port for a sighted person's monitor. The battery is user-replaceable.
