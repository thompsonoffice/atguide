MacinTalk (original)
Company: Apple
Category: TTS Voices
Released: 1984 (MacinTalk 1.1 May 1985)

Overview:
The Macintosh's original speech engine. The Mac used it to introduce itself in 1984 ('Hello. I'm Macintosh.').

Specs:
Maker: Apple
First released: 1984 (MacinTalk 1.1 May 1985)
Synthesis type: Rule-based, phonetic
Platforms: Mac OS 1 to 6
Default on or shipped with: Original Macintosh (1984)
Price: Not documented
Shipped with or used by: outSPOKEN for Macintosh

Physical Description:
Not applicable (software)

Features:
Not documented

History:
Written by Joseph Katz and Mark Barton, who later founded SoftVoice. It had two voices. Apple released MacinTalk 1.1 to developers in May 1985; the Macintosh Repository lists version 1.0.2 dated April 15, 1985, running on Mac OS 1 through 6. Apple notes the original 1984 voice is no longer available.
Successors: MacinTalk 2 (Apple, 1992 to 1994 (v1.2)); MacinTalk Pro (Apple, September 1993 (v1.0); v1.2 February 1994); MacinTalk 3 (Apple, 1994 to 1995 (v1.4 in 1995); standard until Mac OS X)
