Detailed Description:
The Pronto! 18 V3 is a small braille notetaker from BAUM (VisioBraille – BAUM Retec) with an 18-cell display. France's Association Valentin Haüy evaluated it in June 2014 (software 3.6.6, €4,290). It comes in a carrying case worn on a shoulder strap or belt, and is small enough for a large coat pocket.
On the top face, from front to back: a Space key under the right thumb and an Enter key under the left, flanked by small round system buttons (S1 and S2 on the left, S3 and S4 on the right). Behind them are the 18 braille cells with routing keys, then an eight-key braille keyboard. Three round navigation keys (D1–D3 and D4–D6) stand vertically on each side of the cells, and a round, slightly raised speaker sits between dots 1 and 4.
On the front edge, from left to right, are a Record button, a built-in microphone and a five-way joystick (up, down, left, right and a centre press).
On the back edge, from left to right, are the power socket, an SD card reader, a serial port for screen readers, the Bluetooth antenna, a USB-A port for a keyboard or flash drive, a 3.5 mm headphone jack, a 3.5 mm microphone jack and a mini-USB port for ActiveSync with a PC.
It runs Windows CE from 1 GB of flash memory, with about 10 hours of battery life and Nuance speech. Its apps include an editor, calendar, contacts, email, web browser, DAISY and MP3 players, and PDF and Excel readers. It also works as a braille display for a PC or for iOS and Android phones over USB, serial or Bluetooth.

History:
At the 2004 NFB convention HumanWare released the BrailleNote PK, then the smallest notetaker sold in the U.S., with an 18-cell display and speech. According to the NFB timeline its hardware was BAUM's Pronto.
