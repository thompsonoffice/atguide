Hysterical (Apple)
Company: Apple
Category: TTS Voices
Released: MacinTalk 3 (1994)

Overview:
A laughing novelty voice, age 30. Phrase: 'Please stop tickling me!'

Maker: Apple

History:
Added in the 1994 MacinTalk voice set. A macOS novelty voice. It was not on AppleVis's list of novelty voices added to iOS 16, which were Albert, Bad News, Bahh, Bells, Boing, Bubbles, Cellos, Good News, Jester, Organ, Superstar, Trinoids, Whisper, Wobble and Zarvox. René Ros's Talking Eggs utility (version 1.0, June 18, 1994) revealed that each MacinTalk III voice had an age and a joke phrase stored as its comment instead of a description. Hysterical's listed age was 30, and its phrase was "Please stop tickling me!" The readme warned that the voices could sound chopped up and that a Quadra seemed to be needed for them to work properly.
