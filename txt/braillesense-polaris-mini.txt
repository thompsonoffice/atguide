Detailed Description:
The BrailleSense Polaris Mini (2018) is the 20-cell version of the Polaris. It measures 7.28 × 4.05 × 0.90 inches (185 × 103 × 23 mm) and weighs 0.91 pound, small enough for a briefcase or large purse.
The top has the nine-key Perkins keyboard, Control and Alt keys and four function keys. AccessWorld found its keys a bit more clicky than the full Polaris.
Nearest you is a 20-cell braille display with routing buttons and scroll keys.
The right side has a USB-C port for charging, file transfer and braille-display use, with a micro HDMI port toward the back. The left side has volume controls and stereo headphone and microphone jacks. The front panel has the same lock switch, mode switch and media controls as the Polaris.
The speaker and the camera are on the underside. The micro SD slot is inside the battery compartment. Battery life is about 11 hours, and a charge takes about 3.5 hours.

History:
HIMS announced the BrailleSense Polaris Mini a few months after the 32-cell Polaris, which AccessWorld reviewed in January 2018. It is a 20-cell version running Android 5.1.1 on a Samsung Exynos octa-core processor with 64 GB of storage. Unlike the Polaris, it charges over USB-C (about 3.5 hours) and keeps its microSD card in the battery compartment.
Predecessors: BrailleSense U2 Mini (HIMS / Selvas BLV, 2013)
Successors: BrailleSense 6 Mini (HIMS / Selvas BLV, 2022)
