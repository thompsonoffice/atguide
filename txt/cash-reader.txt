History:
Built in Brno because no app could read the Czech koruna. It grew from one currency to ten, then fifty, and its makers say it now covers nearly every banknote, trained on over 10 million photos. AccessWorld's July 2020 review found it supporting nearly 100 currencies, with a 14-day trial, then $0.99 a month, $5.99 a year or $17.99 for life. It offered Siri shortcuts for each currency, optional haptic patterns for each denomination, an automatic flashlight in the dark, low-vision contrast settings and a choice of front or back camera.
