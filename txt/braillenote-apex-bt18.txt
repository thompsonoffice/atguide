BrailleNote Apex BT18
Company: HumanWare
Category: Notetakers
Released: May 2010

Overview:
An 18-cell Apex, launched for the BrailleNote's 10th anniversary. HumanWare called it the tenth notetaker in the line.

Specs:
Released: May 2010
Processor: Freescale i.MX31
Memory (RAM): 256 MB SDRAM
Storage: 8 GB
Operating system: Windows CE 6 with KeySoft
Default speech synthesizer: Eloquence or Keynote Gold
Battery: Rechargeable, user-replaceable lithium-ion
Display or braille cells: 18 cells
Speech synthesizers available: Eloquence (software, with English, Spanish and French voices on the Apex; five selectable Eloquence voices) plus optional KeyNote Gold; chosen in Options > Review Voice
Price at launch: Not documented
Used price today (eBay): Not documented

Physical Description:
2 cm (0.78 in) thick, 734 g (1.8 lb). Another listing gives 0.78 x 9.61 x 5.63 in and 1.6 lb.

Detailed Description:
The BrailleNote Apex BT18 is the 18-cell version of the Apex, launched in May 2010. It shares the Apex design: a thin case in a wraparound leather carrying case.
The top has the eight-key braille keyboard (six dot keys plus Backspace and Enter), with a scroll wheel between the dot 1 and dot 4 keys and the space bar below it.
Nearest you is an 18-cell eight-dot braille display with a cursor routing button above each cell.
Four thumb keys (Previous, Back, Advance and Next) are on the front edge, set right-handed at the factory and switchable to left-handed.
Its ports and user-replaceable battery follow the Apex design. The sources checked give measurements only for the 32-cell Apex, not the 18-cell model.
