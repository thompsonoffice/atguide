History:
One of DECtalk's nine built-in voices. In the DECtalk Software reference (2001, Force Computers), Kit is described as the 'child's voice' and is chosen with the in-line command [:name KIT] or [:nk]. The Speak applet gives it local names: Juanito in Spanish, Karsten in German and Sébastien in French. Its stock settings include an average pitch of 306 Hz, a head size of 80%, breathiness of 47 dB and a pitch range of 210%. Any voice can be reshaped with the Design Voice [:dv] command, and changes can be saved as a tenth voice, Val.
