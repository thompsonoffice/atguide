System Access
Company: Serotek (now Pneuma Solutions)
Category: Screen Readers
Released: December 2005

Overview:
Serotek's low-cost Windows screen reader, which grew out of FreedomBox. It supported Internet Explorer (including DHTML, Ajax and Flash), Outlook, Office, Skype and Adobe Reader.
