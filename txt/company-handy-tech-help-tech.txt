Handy Tech / Help Tech
Type: Company profile
Founded: 1994
Products listed: 4

History:
Siegfried (Sigi) Kipke restarted the company as Handy Tech Elektronik GmbH in Horb, Germany, in 1994 with 8 staff, continuing the braille-display work begun there by Klaus Peter Schönherr, who invented the first braille module in 1975. It was renamed Help Tech GmbH in 2017 and became Help Tech GmbH & Co. KG in 2024. Products include the Braille Wave (1999), Braille Star 40 and 80 (2002), Braillino (2003), Modular Evolution (2006), Active Braille (2011) and Active Star (2015).
