Detailed Description:
Ally Solos Glasses run Envision's Ally AI assistant. They have a classic rectangular frame and weigh about 50 g.
The S/M frame has lenses 51 mm wide and 37 mm tall, a front width of 138 to 149 mm and 163 mm arms.
The camera sits in a hinge (2K-class resolution), with an LED. Whisper open-ear directional speakers let you hear the AI without blocking surrounding sound.
The battery is in quick-swap USB-C charging stems. They run about 10 hours in use, and up to 16 hours claimed. They are IP67-rated.
They connect by Bluetooth 5.2 to the Ally app on iOS or Android and come in a foldable case. The price is $699.
