Jieshuo (Commentary Screen Reader)
Company: Weiliang Li (Jieshuo developer)
Category: Screen Readers
Released: By 2019 (GitHub repository created July 2019); still active

Overview:
A feature-rich Chinese Android screen reader, formerly known as Commentary or TalkMan. Features include a timer, translation, media controls, object and text recognition, and light detection.
