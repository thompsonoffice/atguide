Dolphin Orpheus
Company: Dolphin Computer Access
Category: TTS Voices
Released: Version 1.01 released June 30, 1998

Overview:
Dolphin's software speech synthesizer, the successor to its Apollo hardware synthesizers, used with Hal and Supernova.

Specs:
Maker: Dolphin Computer Access
First released: Version 1.01 released June 30, 1998
Synthesis type: Parallel formant synthesis
Platforms: Windows
Default on or shipped with: Bundled speech for Dolphin Hal and SuperNova
Price: Not documented

Physical Description:
Not applicable (software)

Features:
Not documented

History:
Later versions added sample-based (concatenative) voices alongside the formant voices. Dolphin transferred the Orpheus rights to Meridian One in August 2006, which added SAPI support, more languages and faster speech.
