History:
AT&T Labs launched its Natural Voices Text-to-Speech Engine on July 31, 2001. It grew out of AT&T's unit-selection research (Beutnagel and colleagues, Eurospeech 1999), which picks and joins recorded units from a large speech corpus. It shipped about 20 voices across English variants, French, German, Italian and Spanish for Windows and Linux.
