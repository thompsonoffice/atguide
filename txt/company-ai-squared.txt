Ai Squared

Founded: 1987 (Manchester Center, Vermont)

History:
Maker of ZoomText, which appeared for DOS in 1988 and Windows in 1991. Merged with GW Micro in 2014 and joined VFO in June 2016. ZoomText is now sold by Freedom Scientific.
