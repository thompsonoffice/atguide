Detailed Description:
The OrCam MyEye 2 is a single small bar, about 3 inches long, 0.83 inch high and 0.59 inch thick, that clips magnetically to the arm of a pair of glasses. There's no cable and no separate box: the camera, speaker, battery and controls are all in the bar.
The camera is at the front end, facing forward, with small lights beside it for dim places. The back end, near your ear, has the speaker, the charging port and a small loop for the lanyard. Wear the lanyard: the unit can be knocked off the glasses.
The outer side, facing away from your head, is rounded and has a raised touch bar running along it that clicks when pressed, plus an indicator light. Tap it to read or recognize what's in front of you; swipe forward or back to change the volume or skip through text; double-tap to pause or resume.
The inner side, against the glasses, is flat. It has the magnets that hold it to the mount, the power button near the speaker end, and a second indicator light. A short press of power suspends it; a second press before it suspends turns it off.
The box includes glasses with a mount already fitted, a mount kit with small scissors to fit other frames (on either arm), a charger, a leather case and the lanyard. AccessWorld noted that the small size and touch bar may be hard for anyone with limited dexterity.

History:
OrCam introduced the MyEye 2.0 in December 2017. It was a battery-powered unit about the size of a finger, weighing 22.5 grams (0.8 ounce), that snaps magnetically onto any eyeglass frame. A basic version read text, and an advanced version added face recognition and barcode reading. Wikipedia gave its price in July 2023 as $4,000 to $6,000.
Predecessors: OrCam MyEye (1st generation) (OrCam Technologies, 2015)
Successors: OrCam MyEye 3 Pro (OrCam Technologies, 2024)
