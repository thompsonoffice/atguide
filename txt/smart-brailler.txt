History:
Perkins launched a lighter, quieter Next Generation brailler for students in 2008. It won a 2009 International Design Excellence Award but got a mixed response from braille users, and Wikipedia says it was replaced by the SMART Brailler in 2012. Wikipedia notes that the original brailler's design itself remains unchanged.
Predecessors: Perkins Brailler (Perkins School for the Blind / Perkins Products, 1951 to present)
