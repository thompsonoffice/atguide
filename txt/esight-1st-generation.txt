Detailed Description:
The original eSight, produced commercially from fall 2013, mounts on a prescription-style frame worn like glasses.
At the front is an HD camera, and in front of the eyes are two small near-eye screens showing the enhanced live image.
A wire runs from the headset to a hand-held controller that processes the image and adjusts zoom and contrast.
The controller has a separate battery pack rated at about four hours, carried on a belt or in a pouch. The headset itself has no battery. One user report found it heavier than expected.
It cost $15,000; an introductory 2013 price of $9,750 was also reported.
