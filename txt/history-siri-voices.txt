Siri voices
Company: Apple
Category: Operating system or software version history
Versions listed: 5

Overview:
Siri voice changes since 2011.

Version History:

Version 1: Original voices
Released: October 14, 2011
Notes: Susan Bennett (US), Jon Briggs (UK), Karen Jacobsen (Australia).

Version 2: Male voice option
Released: June 2013
Notes: Changes not listed in the sources cited for this history

Version 3: iOS 11
Released: 2017
Notes: Deep-learning voices.

Version 4: iOS 14.5
Released: April 2021
Notes: Two new American English voices; no default voice.

Version 5: iOS 15.4 beta
Released: February 2022
Notes: Gender-neutral voice (Quinn).

Devices and Software That Ran It:

Siri deep-learning voices (iOS 11) (Apple, 2017 (iOS 11))
Siri voices (iOS 14.5) (Apple, April 2021 (iOS 14.5))
Siri Voice 5 (Quinn) (Apple, February 2022 (iOS 15.4 beta))
