Detailed Description:
AccessWorld described the Victor Reader Classic Plus, the fuller-featured model of the Classic family, in 2003. It is a CD player for DAISY books measuring 9.3 × 8.2 × 2.1 inches and weighing 2.42 pounds. The top slopes upward from front to back.
The built-in speaker is at the top left, with a carrying handle below it. On the right side, from back to front, are the AC socket, a line jack, a remote jack and an earphone jack.
It has 25 keys. Across the top, right of the speaker, are three pairs of keys stacked vertically for tone, volume and speed. Below them is a 12-key telephone-style keypad: 5 has a raised dot, 4 and 6 have raised left and right arrows, and 2 and 8 have fainter up and down arrows.
Below the keypad are Rewind, Play/Stop (square) and Fast Forward (arrow-shaped). The CD Eject key is to the right of Fast Forward, with the Power key above it.
With no CD inside, it enters a Key Describer mode that announces what any key does. The 1 key opens the Bookshelf, 3 the History list, 5 says where you are, 9 sets the sleep timer and 0 gives elapsed and remaining time.

History:
AccessWorld's review of four DAISY book players covered VisuAide's Victor Reader Classic Plus at $375. It measured 9.3 by 8.2 by 2.1 inches, weighed 2.42 pounds, and had a top that slopes up toward the back, a carrying handle, and 25 keys including a phone-style keypad. It took about 10 seconds to load a DAISY CD and could also play music CDs.
Successors: Victor Reader Stream (1st generation) (HumanWare, 2007 to 2014)
