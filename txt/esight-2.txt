Detailed Description:
The eSight 2 (May 2015) is a headset with two HD OLED screens in front of the eyes and a high-resolution camera at the front. 2015 reports call it lightweight but don't give a weight.
You adjust magnification, contrast and color for your vision.
Like the first eSight, it uses a separate controller. The controller holds a swappable battery giving about four hours, and it can stay plugged in to charge while you use the headset.
Its price was about $15,000, with financial help available through eSight.
The sources checked give no exact measurements for the headset.
