Detailed Description:
The BrailleNote mPower QT is the QWERTY-keyboard version of the mPower, introduced in 2005. AccessWorld says the mPower came with either a braille or a QWERTY keyboard, in the same case size as earlier BrailleNotes (about 9½ × 5 × 1 inches).
Instead of the eight braille keys, its top surface carries a typewriter-style QWERTY keyboard. The sources checked don't give a key-by-key layout.
In front of the keyboard is an 18- or 32-cell refreshable braille display with a cursor routing button above each cell, and four thumb keys on the front edge for moving through text.
The ports match the braille-keyboard mPower: power, phone line, serial, CompactFlash and SD slots, two USB host ports and one USB client port on the back; PCMCIA on the right; earphone, power switch, microphone jack and recessed Record button on the left.
The case is silver, and it has the same 128 MB flash storage and Bluetooth as the BT model. A VoiceNote mPower QT version had speech only, with no braille display.

History:
HumanWare introduced the BrailleNote mPower on June 28, 2005, and first showed it at that summer's NFB and ACB conventions. It was the same size as earlier BrailleNotes and came with either a braille or a QWERTY keyboard; the QT models have the QWERTY keyboard. The QT32 cost $6,195 and the QT18 $4,395. Owners of earlier units could have their braille display "transplanted" into a new mPower.
Predecessors: BrailleNote Classic (BT/QT) (Pulse Data International, April 2000 (shipping from June 2000))
Successors: BrailleNote Apex BT32 (HumanWare, November 2009)
