History:
TalkingArch began as Chris Brannon's personal project to make installing Arch Linux with speech easier. With community help and the Archiso tooling, it grew into an unofficial modified Arch install and live image with speech and braille support, also used for rescue and recovery. It aimed to stay as consistent as possible with the official Arch image. ArchiveOS lists New Zealand as its origin, a text-only (CLI) environment, x8664, and a last release dated October 3, 2017.  On April 26, 2010, Chris Brannon announced the April 2010 snapshot of his TalkingArch CD on the arch-general list, describing it as roughly the netinstall CD with speech from boot. He noted improvements since the previous release in fall 2009, including a new x8664 image and a brltty package for braille displays.
