Android (on blindness devices)
Company: Google
Category: Operating system or software version history
Versions listed: 7

Overview:
Google's mobile OS, used by braille notetakers and magnifiers from 2016.

Version History:

Version 1: Android 4.4
Released: 2016
Notes: BrailleNote Touch.

Version 2: Android 5.1.1
Released: 2017
Notes: BrailleSense Polaris.

Version 3: Android 8.1
Released: 2019
Notes: BrailleNote Touch Plus.

Version 4: Android 10 (later 12)
Released: 2021
Notes: BrailleSense 6.

Version 5: Android 12
Released: 2024
Notes: Braille eMotion; current Connect 12 tablet.

Version 6: Android (version not stated in the sources cited)
Released: 2024
Notes: Monarch.

Version 7: Android 15
Released: 2026
Notes: BrailleSense 7.

Devices and Software That Ran It:
BrailleNote Touch (HumanWare, 2016)
BrailleSense Polaris (HIMS / Selvas BLV, 2017)
BrailleSense Polaris Mini (HIMS / Selvas BLV, 2018)
BrailleNote Touch Plus (HumanWare, 2019)
BrailleSense 6 (HIMS / Selvas BLV, 2021)
BrailleSense 6 Mini (HIMS / Selvas BLV, 2022)
Braille eMotion (HIMS / Selvas BLV, 2024)
Connect 12 (HumanWare, 2nd generation 2017; current tablet on Android 12)
Monarch (American Printing House for the Blind (APH), Shipping from September 17, 2024)
BrailleSense 7 (HIMS / Selvas BLV, Unveiled at CSUN, March 2026; shipping from mid/late 2026)
Braille Plus 18 (American Printing House for the Blind (APH), 2012 to February 2015 (discontinued))

System Requirements:
Not documented
