Telesensory Systems (TSI)
Type: Company profile
Founded: 1970
Products listed: 7

History:
Founded in Mountain View, California. It made the Optacon (1971), the Speech+ talking calculator (1976), the VersaBraille (1979) and the Navigator braille displays (1989). It bought Apollo (1984) and Visualtek (1989) and closed in March 2005.

Products:
Optacon (Other, 1971 to 1996)
Speech+ talking calculator (Other, 1976)
VersaBraille (Notetakers, December 1979)
Optacon II (Other, 1985)
VersaBraille II+ (Notetakers, 1987)
TSI Navigator (Braille Displays, 1989)
Soft Vert (Screen Readers, Not documented)
