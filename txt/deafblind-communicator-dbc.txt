DeafBlind Communicator (DBC)
Company: HumanWare
Category: Other
Released: December 2008

Overview:
A BrailleNote with special DB software plus a Bluetooth-linked DBC Companion phone, for face-to-face, TTY and text-message communication.

Specs:
Released: December 2008
Processor: Not documented
Memory (RAM): Not documented
Storage: Not documented
Operating system: Windows CE with KeySoft (BrailleNote base)
Default speech synthesizer: Not documented
Battery: Not documented
Display or braille cells: 18 or 32 cells
Price at launch: $6,195 (18-cell) or $7,995 (32-cell)

Physical Description:
Not documented

Features:
Face-to-face mode with the Companion phone
TTY with answering machine over a landline
SMS and IM with a SIM card

History:
HumanWare began shipping it in December 2008, filling the gap left when the TeleBraille went out of production in 1993.

Ports:
Bluetooth link to the Companion phone; landline TTY connection
