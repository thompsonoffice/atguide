History:
On Stream 3, firmware 1.7 (September 2026) allows up to 2 Acapela voices plus up to 2 eSpeak NG voices. Acapela's roots go back to Babel Technologies, founded in 1997 under Vincent Fontaine as the University of Mons's first spin-off. Babel took over Infovox, a subsidiary of the Swedish operator Telia, in 2001. Babel and the Toulouse text-to-speech company Elan Speech announced their merger on January 7, 2004. Their combined revenue had been €4.7 million in 2003. In April 2004 the group, with Babel Infovox, took the name Acapela Group, with offices in Mons, Toulouse and Stockholm and about 70 staff. Its own timeline lists Infovox Desktop and Virtual Speaker in 2004, Infovox iVox in 2006, an iPhone SDK for Acapela TTS and its first HMM-based TTS project in 2008, and in 2010 Infovox3, a portable talking companion for visually impaired users. Lars-Erik Larsson took over as chief executive from Fontaine in December 2007.
