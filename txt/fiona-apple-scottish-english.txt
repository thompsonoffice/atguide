History:
Part of Lion's new downloadable voices. In March 2011, before Lion shipped, AppleInsider reported that Apple would offer 53 new high-quality voices in more than 40 languages and dialects. Each was about a third to more than half a gigabyte, so they came as optional downloads instead of being preinstalled. They joined Alex (Leopard, 2007) and Vicki (Panther, 2003). The new English voices were Tom, Samantha and Jill (US); Karen and Lee (Australia); Daniel, Emily and Serena (UK); Fiona (Scotland); Sangeeta (India); Moira (Ireland); and Tessa (South Africa). 9to5Mac later identified the voices as Nuance speech technology, the same company that powered Siri. To install one: System Preferences, Speech, Text to Speech, System Voice, Customize. Fiona was the only Scottish English voice.
