MacinTalk 3
Company: Apple
Category: TTS Voices
Released: 1994 to 1995 (v1.4 in 1995); standard until Mac OS X

Overview:
Apple's text-to-speech standard in System 7, Mac OS 8 and Mac OS 9, with 19 voices including Fred, Kathy, Ralph, Junior, Princess and the novelty voices.

Specs:
Maker: Apple
First released: 1994 to 1995 (v1.4 in 1995); standard until Mac OS X
Synthesis type: Not described in the sources searched (CMU comp.speech FAQ, Speech Manager notes). The same survey labels MacinTalk 2 and MacinTalk Pro concatenative but gives no method for MacinTalk 3.
Platforms: System 7, Mac OS 8 and 9 (68030 at 33 MHz, about 300 KB of memory)
Default on or shipped with: Mac OS 7 to 9 (Fred and others)
Price: Not documented
Voices: Fred (Apple); Kathy (Apple); Ralph (Apple); Junior (Apple); Princess (Apple); Zarvox (Apple); Whisper (Apple); Bells (Apple); Boing (Apple); Bubbles (Apple); Cellos (Apple); Albert (Apple); Bad News (Apple); Good News (Apple); Trinoids (Apple); Hysterical (Apple); Pipe Organ (Apple); Deranged (Apple); Bahh (Apple)

Physical Description:
Not applicable (software)

Features:
Not documented

History:
The voices shipped with Speech Manager 1.2 in 1994. Each voice stored a joke phrase as its comment, revealed by the 1994 Talking Eggs utility. Fred later survived byte-identical into Mac OS X. One 1994 report said a Quadra was needed for smooth speech. René Ros's Talking Eggs utility (version 1.0, June 18, 1994) revealed that each MacinTalk III voice had an age and a joke phrase stored as its comment instead of a description. Fred's was "I sure like being inside this fancy computer," Kathy's "Isn't it nice to have a computer that will talk to you?" and Zarvox's "That looks like a peaceful planet."
Predecessors: MacinTalk 2 (Apple, 1992 to 1994 (v1.2))
Successors: Mac OS X Speech Synthesis (MacinTalk in OS X) (Apple, Mac OS X (2001) onward)
