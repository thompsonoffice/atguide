History:
One of DECtalk's nine built-in voices. In the DECtalk Software reference (2001, Force Computers), Wendy is described as the 'whispering female voice' and is chosen with the in-line command [:name WENDY] or [:nw]. The Speak applet gives it local names: Wendy in Spanish, Wendy in German and Nadia in French. Its stock settings include an average pitch of 200 Hz, a head size of 100%, breathiness of 55 dB and a pitch range of 175%. Any voice can be reshaped with the Design Voice [:dv] command, and changes can be saved as a tenth voice, Val.
