Detailed Description:
The BrailleSense U2 QWERTY (model H432Q) is the typewriter-keyboard version of the U2. Dealer and HIMS data give 250 × 160 × 20 mm (9.84 × 6.3 × 0.78 in) and 838 g (1.84 lb).
Instead of the Perkins braille keys, its top carries a standard QWERTY keyboard for typing as on a computer.
Nearest you is a 32-cell braille display with 32 routing keys and four scroll keys, two at each end.
The front has five audio buttons for the media player. It has USB and SD connections, GPS and a vibration motor.
It runs Windows CE 6.0 with 32 GB of storage and a detachable battery. It is discontinued; HIMS points buyers to the BrailleSense 6.
