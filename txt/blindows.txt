History:
Blindows was the Windows screen reader of the German company Frank Audiodata, later Audiodata GmbH. Die Welt covered it in February 1999 as a way for blind users to browse the web. After BAUM bought Frank Audiodata in 2004, BAUM had two quite different screen readers, Blindows and its own VIRGO, and the two teams began a joint successor. Development of both ended in 2006, and COBRA replaced them.
Successors: COBRA (BAUM Retec, November 2008 (COBRA 8) onward)
