HIMS / Selvas BLV
Type: Company profile
Founded: Not documented
Products listed: 28

History:
A Korean maker of the Braille Hansone (2002), BrailleSense (2004), VoiceSense (2007), Braille EDGE, SmartBeetle, BookSense, Polaris, QBraille XL, BrailleSense 6 (2021), Braille eMotion (2024) and BrailleSense 7 (2026). GW Micro distributed its products in the US from about 2006 to 2010. In September 2014 Diotek (later Selvas AI) agreed to buy 87.8% of HIMS International. Its founding year is not documented in the sources found.
