HIMS / Selvas BLV
Type: Company profile
Founded: 1999 (Korea; after a teacher at a Korean school for the blind asked for a notetaker). North American arm renamed Selvas North America in May 2024.
Products listed: 28

History:
A Korean maker of the Braille Hansone (2002), BrailleSense (2004), VoiceSense (2007), Braille EDGE, SmartBeetle, BookSense, Polaris, QBraille XL, BrailleSense 6 (2021), Braille eMotion (2024) and BrailleSense 7 (2026). GW Micro distributed its products in the US from about 2006 to 2010. In September 2014 Diotek (later Selvas AI) agreed to buy 87.8% of HIMS International. in the sources found. HIMS was founded in 1999 after a teacher at a Korean school for the blind asked it to build a notetaker for blind students.
