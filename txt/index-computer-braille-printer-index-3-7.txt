History:
Index began in 1979 as a university project to build a braille typewriter with a copy function. Björn Löfstedt was inspired by his mother, who went blind at 32. With support from a Swedish disability organization, he and Torvald Lundqvist founded Polar Print Production (later Index Braille AB) in 1982. A small batch of Index Computer Braille Printers was built in Löfstedt's garage in 1984; they became known as the 'Index 3.7' after the firmware version. Index moved into its current premises in 1985, paid for by distributing assistive technology around Sweden.
Successors: Index Blue Bar (Index Braille, 1988)
