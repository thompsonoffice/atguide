Detailed Description:
The BrailleNote Touch Plus (2018–2019) is the faster refresh of the BrailleNote Touch, in the same body: 0.8 inch high, 9½ inches wide, 6.3 inches deep and about 2 pounds. AccessWorld called it 'a bit on the hefty side'.
Like the original, it has a glass touch screen for TouchBraille typing and a case with a built-in Perkins-style braille keyboard that folds over the screen.
Nearest you is the braille display, 18 or 32 cells in the same size case, with routing buttons and four thumb keys on the front edge.
The power button is more recessed than on the original Touch, to stop it switching on in a bag. It charges and connects through USB-C instead of micro USB.
Its accessibility software is built into the device instead of a removable internal SD card. It ships with the Acapela Sharon voice, and other voices can be added.

History:
It launched in February 2019 with Android 8.1. A September 2025 update (after about three years without one) renamed KeyMath to KeyGraph and added KeyRecorder, according to user reports; the device stayed on Android 8.1.
Predecessors: BrailleNote Touch (HumanWare, 2016)
Successors: BrailleNote Evolve (HumanWare, Previewed late 2025; launched 2026)
