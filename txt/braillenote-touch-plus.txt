BrailleNote Touch Plus
Company: HumanWare
Category: Notetakers
Released: 2019

Overview:
An updated Touch with a much faster processor and newer Android.

Specs:
Released: 2019
Processor: Qualcomm Snapdragon 820, quad-core Kryo 2.2 GHz
Memory (RAM): 4 GB LPDDR4
Storage: 64 GB flash
Operating system: Android 8.1 Oreo with KeySoft
Default speech synthesizer: Not documented
Battery: Not documented
Display or braille cells: 18 or 32 cells
Price at launch: Not documented

Physical Description:
Not documented

Features:
21 MP camera
TalkBack-based accessibility

History:
It launched in February 2019 with Android 8.1. A September 2025 update (after about three years without one) renamed KeyMath to KeyGraph and added KeyRecorder, according to user reports; the device stayed on Android 8.1.
Predecessors: BrailleNote Touch (HumanWare, 2016)
Successors: BrailleNote Evolve (HumanWare, Announced November 2025, launched 2026)

Ports:
USB-C (USB 3.0 speed), HDMI with audio, Wi-Fi a/b/g/n/ac 2x2, Bluetooth 4.2

Operating System:
Android 8.1 Oreo with KeySoft
Operating system history: KeySoft
Operating system history: Android (on blindness devices)

System Requirements:
Not documented
