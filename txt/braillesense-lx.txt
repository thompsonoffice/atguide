BrailleSense LX
Company: HIMS / Selvas BLV
Category: BrailleSense
Released: 2008

Overview:
A 2008 BrailleSense variant. HIMS lists it as its 2007 to 2008 notetaker, between the original BrailleSense (2004) and the BrailleSense OnHand (2010). Neither HIMS nor Selvas BLV publishes a spec sheet for it.

Detailed Description:
The BrailleSense LX is a HIMS braille notetaker that the company's official product history lists under 2008.
In that history it comes after the original BrailleSense (2004) and before the BrailleSense OnHand (2010). HIMS is now Selvas BLV.
Searches for a BrailleSense LX spec sheet, user manual, brochure, dealer listing or review all came up empty. The HIMS and Selvas history pages give only its name, year and product photo slot.
The NVDA screen reader's HIMS driver uses the device ID 'LX' for a BrailleSense model with four scroll keys. Nothing in the driver connects that ID to this product name, though, so no physical details are taken from it.
Its cell count, keyboard type, ports, battery, size and weight are not included, because no source found in this research documents them.
