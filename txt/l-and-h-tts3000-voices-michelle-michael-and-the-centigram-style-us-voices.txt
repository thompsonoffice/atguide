L&H TTS3000 voices (Michelle, Michael and the Centigram-style US voices)
Company: Lernout & Hauspie
Category: TTS Voices
Released: Late 1990s (L&H versions 6.03 to 6.13)

Overview:
Lernout & Hauspie's multilingual SAPI 4 engine. Its US English voices were Adult Female 1 (Michelle) and Adult Male 1 (Michael), plus Peter, Sidney, Eager Eddie, Deep Douglas, Biff, Grandpa Amos, Melvin and Alex, which Genesys documentation says sound like Centigram's voices of the same names.
