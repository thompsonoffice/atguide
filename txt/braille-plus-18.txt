Braille Plus 18
Company: American Printing House for the Blind (APH)
Category: PDAs
Released: 2012 to February 2015 (discontinued)

Overview:
APH's Android device with an 18-cell, 8-dot braille display, billed as the world's first Android smartphone designed for blind students and professionals.

Specs:
Released: 2012 to February 2015 (discontinued)
Processor: Not documented
Memory (RAM): Not documented
Storage: Not documented
Operating system: Android
Default speech synthesizer: Not documented
Battery: 4 to 6 hours to charge; about a full day of use
Display or braille cells: 18 cells, 8-dot
Speech synthesizers available: Not documented
Price at launch: $3,599 (2012)
Used price today (eBay): Not documented

Physical Description:
Under 6.5 x 4.1 in and just over 1 in thick.

Features:
18-cell 8-dot braille display
5 MP camera
GPS, Wi-Fi and 3G

History:
The FCC honored it for innovation in communications technology in 2014. APH discontinued it in February 2015.
Predecessors: APH Braille+ Mobile Manager (American Printing House for the Blind (APH), May 2007 to 2012)
