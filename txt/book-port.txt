Book Port
Company: American Printing House for the Blind (APH)
Category: Book Readers
Released: May 2003

Overview:
APH's handheld DAISY and text reader with 18 keys, running on 2 AA batteries.

Specs:
Released: May 2003
Processor: Not documented
Memory (RAM): Not documented
Storage: Not documented
Operating system: Not documented
Default speech synthesizer: DoubleTalk
Battery: 2 AA batteries
Display or braille cells: Not documented
Price at launch: Not documented

Physical Description:
4 x 2.25 x 1 in, 6 oz with batteries. An 18-key keypad: a telephone keypad with a nib on 5, plus keys A to F. Headphone jack and USB port on top, card slot on the right, battery compartment and belt clip on the back.

Features:
DAISY, text, Word, HTML, BRF and MP3
DoubleTalk synthesizer
Voice memo recorder
Remembers the reading position

History:
Debuted in May 2003. Firmware 1.2 shipped in September 2004 (APH revision history).
Successors: Book Port Plus (American Printing House for the Blind (APH), March 10, 2010 (APH availability announcement))

Ports:
USB, headphone jack, flash memory card slot
