History:
One of DECtalk's nine built-in voices. Voice parameters such as throat size were editable. In the DECtalk Software reference (2001, Force Computers), Betty is described as the 'full female voice' and is chosen with the in-line command [:name BETTY] or [:nb]. The Speak applet gives it local names: Berta in Spanish, Beate in German and Marjolaine in French. Its stock settings include an average pitch of 208 Hz, a head size of 100%, breathiness of 0 dB and a pitch range of 240%. Any voice can be reshaped with the Design Voice [:dv] command, and changes can be saved as a tenth voice, Val.
