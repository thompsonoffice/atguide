Detailed Description:
Envision Glasses look like a band across your face: a thin titanium frame on the left and a wider black Glass body on the right. They come assembled in a protective carrying case.
The Glass body forms the right arm and holds all the electronics, about 45 g. At its back end are the USB-C port, the power button and the speaker.
Along the outside of the right arm, against your temple, is the touchpad for taps and swipes.
The body folds forward in front of your right eye. That front part holds a small display that can be switched off and the camera. Moving your finger along the body, you feel an indent at the hinge, which you press for voice commands.
The titanium frame makes up the bridge and left arm and attaches with round metal hinges. Smith Optics frames with swappable lenses can be ordered.
