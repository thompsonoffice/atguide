BrailleNote Classic (BT/QT)
Company: Pulse Data International
Category: Notetakers
Released: April 2000 (shipping from June 2000)

Overview:
The first BrailleNote, a Windows CE-based notetaker running KeySoft, made by Pulse Data.

Specs:
Released: April 2000 (shipping from June 2000)
Processor: MIPS R4000 at 100 MHz
Memory (RAM): 4 MB, expandable to 16 MB (flash for OS and data, plus RAM)
Storage: Not documented
Operating system: Windows CE 2.12, later up to CE.NET 4.2, running KeySoft
Default speech synthesizer: Keynote Gold Multimedia, running under Windows CE
Battery: Rechargeable NiMH
Display or braille cells: 18 or 32 cells with touch cursors
Speech synthesizers available: Not documented
Price at launch: BrailleNote 18: $3,395; BrailleNote 32: $4,995 (2000)
Used price today (eBay): Not documented
Voices: Keynote Gold

Physical Description:
9.5 x 5 x 1 in. The 32-cell model weighs 2 lb 9 oz. Gray case with green keys; the 18- and 32-cell models share one case, and an 18-cell unit can be upgraded to 32 cells.

Features:
KeySoft suite: KeyWord, KeyPlan, KeyList, KeyMail
Braille (BT) or QWERTY (QT) keyboard
Touch cursors above each cell
Four thumb keys
Internal soft modem
Speaker about 2 inches across
Can work as a braille display and synthesizer with Window-Eyes
ActiveSync with a PC
English only at launch

History:
Released in April 2000 and shipping by June 2000 (AccessWorld review). It competed with the Braille Lite 2000 (18 cells, $3,395) and Braille Lite 40 2000 ($5,495). Sendero GPS launched on it in 2001. KeySoft 7.5 was its last software release; KeySoft 8.0 did not support it.
Predecessors: Keynote talking word processor (Pulse Data International, 1986)
Successors: BrailleNote PK (Pulse Data International, Announced July 2004); BrailleNote mPower BT (HumanWare, June 2005)

Ports:
PCMCIA slot, serial, parallel, modem, infrared, earphone jack, AC adapter

Operating System:
Windows CE 2.12, later up to CE.NET 4.2, running KeySoft
Operating system history: KeySoft
Operating system history: Windows CE / Windows Mobile (on notetakers and PDAs)

System Requirements:
Not documented
