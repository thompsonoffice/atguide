Detailed Description:
The VarioUltra is a very thin braille display and basic note-taker from BAUM, in 20- and 40-cell sizes. The 20-cell model is 7.4 by 3.5 by 0.7 inches and weighs 0.7 pounds; the 40-cell model is 12.4 inches long and weighs 1.1 pounds. Reviewers describe it as slim and well built.
The front of the top is sloped at about 30 degrees. On that slope, from left to right, are system keys S1 and S2, a spacebar, a round joystick called the NaviStick, a second spacebar, and system keys S3 and S4. The NaviStick also presses down to select.
Behind these is the braille display, with three buttons stacked in a vertical line at each end for panning and other functions. Behind the display are the cursor-routing keys, and at the back is a standard eight-key braille keyboard.
The right side has only the power button, just behind the front edge. The left side has, from front to back, a keyboard lock switch, a switch between note-taker and display modes, a micro-USB port and a full-size USB port. The back edge is empty. The two front corners have attachment points for a shoulder strap.
It came in a leather case that closes with magnets and lets you use the display while carrying it by the strap.

History:
Made by BAUM Retec AG in 20- and 40-cell sizes. Built-in apps include a word processor, PDF and spreadsheet viewers, a calculator, timers and an alarm clock. It has no SD slot; files move over USB, with the unit appearing as a drive on a PC. AccessWorld compared the VarioUltra 40 with the HIMS Braille Edge 40 ($2,795) in 2017, finding the VarioUltra lighter and slimmer but over $1,000 dearer.
