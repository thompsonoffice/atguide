Nuance Communications
Type: Company profile
Founded: 1992 (as Visioneer; renamed ScanSoft 1999; merged with Nuance and renamed Nuance Communications in October 2005)
Products listed: 5

History:
Through acquisitions, including SpeechWorks (which bought Eloquent Technology in January 2001), Rhetorical (2004) and SVOX (2011), it owned the Eloquence, RealSpeak and Vocalizer speech engines. It also made the TALKS and ZOOMS software for Symbian phones.

Products:
Nuance TALKS (and ZOOMS) (Screen Readers, By 2004 to about 2013)
Nuance Daniel (TTS Voices, By 2016 (updated in Vocalizer Expressive 2.2))
Nuance Samantha (TTS Voices, By 2016 (updated in Vocalizer Expressive 2.2))
Nuance Tom (TTS Voices, By 2016 (updated in Vocalizer Expressive 2.2))
Nuance Vocalizer / RealSpeak (engine) (TTS Voices, Mid-2000s to present. Nuance built its TTS line through acquisitions, including Rhetorical (2004) and SVOX (2011), and Vocalizer was the TTS in the first-generation Victor Reader Stream (2007). The first release date of the Vocalizer brand itself was not found.)
