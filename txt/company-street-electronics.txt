Street Electronics
Type: Company profile
Founded: 1978 (Milo Street, Carpinteria, California)
Products listed: 1

History:
Street Electronics Corporation of Carpinteria, California, made the Echo II speech card for the Apple II (manual copyright 1982), with its Textalker and Speakeasy software. Its trademarks also included Echo GP.

Products:
Echo II (Speech Synthesizers, 1982)
