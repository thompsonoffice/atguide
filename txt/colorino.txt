Detailed Description:
The Colorino is a handheld talking color identifier from the Austrian company Caretec. You hold its tip against clothing, a wall or any surface and it speaks the color in plain language. It recognizes over 150 shades.
It is a small bar about 110 mm long, 52 mm wide and 25 mm thick, and weighs 84 grams with its batteries. The body is black, red and yellow.
It works with just two keys. A built-in loudspeaker has three volume levels, and a 3.5 mm headphone jack lets you hear results privately.
It also detects light, sounding an audio signal (0 to 2.5 kHz, per Caretec's spec table) that shows how bright a light source is, so you can tell whether a lamp is on or find a window. Caretec says it can tell natural from artificial light and measure contrast and brightness.
It runs on two 1.5 V AAA batteries. In the box are the device, batteries, a textile case and printed instructions.

History:
Caretec markets the Colorino as already in daily use by many people around the world. It sits below Caretec's Color Star and Color Star Pro, which name more than 1,700 shades.  Dialogue magazine's November–December 2006 gift guide listed the Colorino Talking Color Identifier from APH at $195, in English (1-03955-00) or Spanish (1-03955-SP). The guide says it detects more than 100 nuances of colour, also works as a light detector, and comes with two AAA batteries, a carrying case, a cassette, and large-print and braille quick-start guides.
