U.S. Bureau of Engraving and Printing (BEP)
Type: Company profile
Founded: Founding year not covered by the pages used here
Products listed: 1

History:
The U.S. Treasury bureau that prints Federal Reserve notes. Until redesigned currency with tactile features arrives, it gives eligible blind and visually impaired people a free iBill reader (AccessWorld, June 2015), and it publishes the free EyeNote app.

Products:
EyeNote (BEP money identifier app) (Miscellaneous, 2011 (iOS); EyeNote 4.0 on August 16, 2024; later released for Android)
