History:
Developer Jonathan Nadeau, blind since a 1992 car accident, launched the Sonar Project on Indiegogo in early 2013, seeking $20,000 to build a free, Ubuntu-based Linux distribution for blind, low-vision, dyslexic and motor-impaired users. PCWorld noted on February 4, 2013 that it had raised $2,385. Sonar 13.04 was announced on the Orca list in April 2013, and Sonar 2014 in July 2014. In March 2017 Sonar GNU/Linux merged with Vinux.
