BrailleNote mPower BT
Company: HumanWare
Category: Notetakers
Released: June 2005

Overview:
The second-generation full-size BrailleNote, with braille keyboard.

Specs:
Released: June 2005
Processor: Intel XScale 416 MHz
Memory (RAM): Not documented
Storage: 128 MB flash (DiskOnChip)
Operating system: Windows CE with KeySoft 6.2 at launch
Default speech synthesizer: Not documented
Battery: Not documented
Display or braille cells: 18 or 32 cells
Price at launch: Not documented

Physical Description:
Not documented

Features:
SD, CompactFlash and PCMCIA slots
Bluetooth

History:
Launched alongside the VoiceNote mPower. It ran KeySoft 6.2 at launch; KeySoft 7.5 Build 29 (May 2008) added 802.11g Wi-Fi card support, and KeySoft 8.0 followed in 2009.
Predecessors: BrailleNote Classic (BT/QT) (Pulse Data International, April 2000 (shipping from June 2000))
Successors: BrailleNote Apex BT32 (HumanWare, November 2009)

2 USB host ports plus mini-USB client; SD, CF and PCMCIA slots; Bluetooth

Windows CE with KeySoft 6.2 at launch
