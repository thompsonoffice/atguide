Detailed Description:
The BrailleNote mPower, introduced June 28, 2005, has the same size and keyboard as earlier BrailleNotes (about 9½ × 5 × 1 inches). It looks sleeker, with a silver case instead of gray and forest-green keys instead of teal.
The top has the curved eight-dot braille keyboard with space bar and an 18- or 32-cell braille display with a routing button above each cell. The four thumb keys stay on the front edge facing you.
The back panel has the power socket, a telephone jack for the built-in 56K modem, a serial port, a CompactFlash slot, an SD card slot, two USB host ports, one USB client port and built-in Bluetooth. There is no parallel port.
The PCMCIA slot is on the right side. On the left side are the earphone jack and power switch, plus a new external microphone jack and a small recessed Record button that starts a voice recording from any application. An internal microphone is built in.
Inside is a 128 MB flash drive. AccessWorld noted excellent stereo sound through headphones, while the built-in speaker is the same modest one as earlier models.
