RC Systems
Type: Company profile
Founded: Incorporated July 26, 1983 (Everett, Washington)
Products listed: 2

History:
Maker of the DoubleTalk PC (an 8-bit ISA card) and the DoubleTalk LT (RS-232, using the RC8650 chipset). DoubleTalk was the first synthesizer supported by the Speakup Linux screen reader in 1998.
