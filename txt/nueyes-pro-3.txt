Detailed Description:
The NuEyes Pro 3, announced December 15, 2020, is a lightweight black binocular augmented-reality headset weighing 88 g. It is tethered by cable, with no built-in battery mentioned.
It has two 4K displays with a wide field of view of about 51 to 52 degrees.
The main version connects to a proprietary controller with two motion-tracking cameras and a forward-facing 20-megapixel camera.
The Pro 3-C version has a 5-megapixel camera and a USB-C plug for phones, tablets and laptops that output video. A third version is for thermal imaging.
It went on sale December 18, 2020. A UK retailer listed it at £3,995.
