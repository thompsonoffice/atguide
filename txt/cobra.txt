History:
COBRA, made by BAUM Retec AG of Wiesenbach, Germany, is the joint successor to Blindows and BAUM's VIRGO. COBRA 8 came out in November 2008 and 8.1 in April 2009, with braille and speech only, still using BAUM's WebFormator add-on for Internet Explorer to read web pages. COBRA 9 (January 2010) added magnification and replaced WebFormator with a built-in COBRA Explorer. Version 9.1 came in December 2010, 10 in November 2012 (Windows XP to 7) and 11 in May 2016 (Windows 8 and 10). Features include OCR of images, text snippets, splitting braille displays and running more than one at once, and VBScript scripting. A 2011 Aktion Mensch article named it as JAWS's runner-up in Germany. After BAUM went insolvent, VisioBraille took over its assets and still offers COBRA downloads and license activation.
Predecessors: Blindows (BAUM Retec, In use by February 1999; development ended 2006)
