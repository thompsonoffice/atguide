Index Braille
Type: Company profile
Founded: 1982
Products listed: 9

History:
It began as a 1979 university project to build a braille typewriter with a copy function. Björn Löfstedt and Torvald Lundqvist founded it in Luleå, Sweden as Polar Print Production, and the first embossers were built in Björn's garage in 1984. Product platforms followed in 1992 (Everest), 1995 (V2), 1998 (4X4 Pro), 2002 to 2004 (V3), 2005 (4Waves Pro), 2011 (V4) and 2016 (V5).
