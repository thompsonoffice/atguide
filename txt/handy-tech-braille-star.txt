History:
Handy Tech introduced the Braille Star 40 and Braille Star 80 in 2002. It described the Star 40 as ideal for use with a laptop, and the Star 80 as a system for professional workplaces, particularly blind telephone operators. The Braille Star 40 gained Bluetooth in 2004.
Successors: Handy Tech Active Star (Handy Tech / Help Tech, 2015)
