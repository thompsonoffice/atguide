Detailed Description:
The BookSense XT is physically identical to the standard BookSense but adds an FM radio, 4 GB of built-in memory and Bluetooth headset support. GW Micro priced it at $499.
It is a candy-bar-shaped player, 4.25 × 1.85 × 0.73 inches and 4 ounces. Stereo speakers are at the top of the face, above a date-and-time button, a five-way control and a phone-style keypad with alternating raised and recessed keys.
The right side has Play/Pause (also power), Record and a lock switch. The left side has the Mode button, which on the XT also switches to the FM radio, plus volume keys and an SD slot. Microphone and headphone jacks are on top, and power and USB on the bottom.
AccessWorld tested an XT with software 1.1. It recommended the XT for anyone wanting a built-in FM radio, 4 GB of memory, Word document reading and the smallest size.
Users report MP3 or WAV recording, a 12-hour battery and voice-command control of playback.
