Detailed Description:
The SmartView Graduate is a HumanWare video magnifier that connects to a laptop and has no screen of its own; the magnified image appears on the computer. Assembled, it measures 12 × 9 × 15.5 in (VisionAid's metric figure is 22.8 × 30 × 39 cm) and weighs 2.5 lb (1.1 kg).
It breaks down into three main parts (the camera, the arm and the base), which fit in a laptop bag or an optional travel case. Setup takes under a minute.
The colour autofocus camera zooms continuously from about 3× to 50× and rotates 360°, so it can switch between reading on the desk and viewing a board across the room.
The included software on the PC provides zoom control, adjustable contrast and brightness, high-contrast viewing modes, image rotation, freeze-frame, and image capture saved to the computer.
It draws all its power from the laptop over two USB 2.0 cables, so no mains adapter is needed. It came with USB cables, a software and documentation CD, and an installation guide.

History:
The UK dealer VisionAid lists the Graduate as discontinued; it was priced at £1,750 there with VAT relief. VisionAid described it as a laptop-connected magnifier for students and travelling professionals: it splits into three parts that fit in a computer bag, sets up in under a minute, and takes its power from the laptop through two USB 2.0 cables. VisionAid lists compatibility with Windows XP, Vista, 7 and 8. No dated launch announcement was found, so the introduction year remains unknown.
