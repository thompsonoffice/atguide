Detailed Description:
The eSight 3 (February 2017) is a lightweight headset; reports give 104 g for the headset.
It has two OLED displays in front of the eyes and a 21.5-megapixel camera, with up to 24× zoom.
Reports from 2017 describe a separate handheld controller that carries the battery, connected to the headset by cable. Battery life is not documented in the sources used.
Its launch price is not documented in the sources used.
