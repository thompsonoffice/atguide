RHVoice
Company: Olga Yakovleva (RHVoice)
Category: TTS Voices
Released: Source copyright from 2010; public NVDA driver 0.2.7 on April 29, 2012

Overview:
A free, open-source speech synthesizer by Olga Yakovleva. It started with Russian male and female voices and grew to other languages. It runs as an NVDA add-on, a Windows SAPI 5 engine, an Android app, a Linux speech-dispatcher module and a command-line tool.

History:
In February 2012 a patch added an RHVoice output module to speech-dispatcher on Linux. It described RHVoice as the only free high-quality Russian synthesizer and planned Ukrainian, Belarusian and Kazakh voices. The NVDA synth driver 0.2.7 was dated April 29, 2012. Python packages followed in 2015.
