VoiceView versions
Company: Amazon
Category: Operating system or software version history
Versions listed: 3

Overview:
Amazon's Fire tablet screen reader, a full rewrite that replaced the TalkBack integration on earlier Fire tablets.

Version History:

Version 1: Before VoiceView
Released: 2011 to 2015
Notes: Fire tablets used an integration of Android TalkBack.

Version 2: VoiceView 1 (Fire OS 5 'Bellini')
Released: September 2015
Notes: Linear and touch navigation, IVONA voices, a wide speed range, lower latency, simplified gestures, new earcons, a jog-wheel gesture, a tutorial, and braille through BrailleBack.

Version 3: Braille update
Released: September 2017
Notes: Native Bluetooth braille input and output in contracted, uncontracted, UEB and computer braille, with chord commands; BAUM, HumanWare and Orbit Reader 20 displays.

Devices and Software That Ran It:
VoiceView (Amazon Fire tablets) (Amazon, September 2015 (Fire OS 5 'Bellini'))

System Requirements:
An Amazon Fire tablet running Fire OS 5 or later (Fire OS 5 is based on Android 5.1.1). Braille needs a 4th-generation or newer Fire tablet and a supported Bluetooth display.
