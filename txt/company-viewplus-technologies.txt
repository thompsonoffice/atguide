ViewPlus Technologies
Type: Company profile
Founded: 1996 (John Gardner, Corvallis, Oregon)
Products listed: 3

History:
Physicist John Gardner went blind in 1988. By 1996 his Oregon State University team had developed TIGER multi-height tactile graphics, and John and Carolyn Gardner started ViewPlus to sell it. Products include the Tiger Advantage (2000), Emprint (2006), VP Max (2009 to 2021) and the later Columbia, SpotDot, Premier and Elite embossers.

Products:
