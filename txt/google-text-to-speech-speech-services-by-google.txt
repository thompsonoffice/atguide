Google Text-to-Speech (Speech Services by Google)
Company: Google
Category: TTS Voices
Released: October 2013 on Google Play (reported November 6, 2013)

Overview:
Google's Android speech engine, delivered as a Play Store app so voices can update without a new Android release. It speaks for TalkBack, Google Play Books and Google Translate.
