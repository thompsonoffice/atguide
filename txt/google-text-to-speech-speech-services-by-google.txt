Google Text-to-Speech (Speech Services by Google)
Company: Google
Category: TTS Voices
Released: October 2013 on Google Play (reported November 6, 2013)

Overview:
Google's Android speech engine, delivered as a Play Store app so voices can update without a new Android release. It speaks for TalkBack, Google Play Books and Google Translate.

History:
Android Police noted the Play Store app could replace the weak default voices on phones such as the Galaxy Note 3. The app was later renamed Speech Services by Google (Speech Recognition and Synthesis), which passed 10 billion Play Store downloads in November 2021.
Predecessors: SVOX Pico (Android) (SVOX, September 2009 (Android 1.6 Donut))
