Detailed Description:
The HIMS GoVision Pro is an all-in-one video magnifier with its monitor and camera arm built together. Ergoguys lists it at 460 mm wide, 570 mm high and 180 mm deep (18.1 × 22.4 × 7.1 in), weighing 8.5 kg (18.7 lb).
A camera on an arm in front of the screen magnifies from 1.7× to 68× in Near View and rotates 360° for Distance View and Self View. LED lights with brightness control light the reading area, and there is a built-in guide for the reading table.
For portability you turn the camera arm and head parallel to the screen and lift one lever, which folds them down behind the monitor.
You control it with a remote that has large, high-contrast buttons and speaks the item you are on, or with a jog switch on the side of the camera. There are 7 colour modes (4 user-definable, 57 combinations), focus lock, line markers, a built-in speaker, an earphone jack and volume control.
A separate 13-megapixel camera captures a full letter-size page for OCR with text-to-speech, with a guide frame, column reading and automatic language detection. It also includes a document viewer, a video player with subtitles, a photo frame, a clock and a screen saver.

History:
HIMS lists the GoVision Pro as a 2018 product in its official product history. It is an all-in-one, transportable HD video magnifier that combines desktop magnification with a 13-megapixel camera for full-page OCR and speech, a 360° rotating camera for distance viewing such as a classroom whiteboard, and a self-view mode for grooming. The reseller Ergoguys lists its specifications as 460 × 570 × 180 mm and 8.5 kg, with near-view magnification of 1.7× to 68×. It also includes a document viewer, a video player and a digital photo frame.
Predecessors: GoVision (HIMS / Selvas BLV, 2016)
