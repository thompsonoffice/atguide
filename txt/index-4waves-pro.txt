Detailed Description:
The 4Waves PRO is a floor-standing, high-volume production embosser for tractor-fed paper. It measures 169 by 70 by 114 cm and weighs 195 kg; the shipping weight is 264 kg, on a pallet. Index called it the first embosser built on a multi-printer design.
Inside are four identical embossing modules working together, which give up to 1,000 pages per hour. Each module measures 58 by 23 by 19 cm and weighs 8.7 kg. A fifth service module comes with it. When a module needs maintenance, you swap in the spare in a few minutes without tools.
The machine adapts automatically to running on three, two or one modules, so it keeps embossing while at least one module works. At power-on it confirms each module by speech: check module, one wave, two wave, three wave, four wave, ready.
Like other Index embossers, it has a speech-equipped control panel with print-and-braille keys. Unlike the other V3 models, it has no 9-pin serial port; it connects by USB, network and parallel. It can restart a job at the point where it stopped after a paper jam.
It embosses high-resolution tactile graphics anywhere in a document using the WinBraille editor. Its warranty was 2 years or 5,000,000 pages, compared with 200,000 for Index's smaller embossers.

History:
Introduced in 2005, the 4Waves Pro was Index's high-speed production embosser. It came between the V3 platform (2002 to 2004) and the V4 platform (2011).
