Perkins School for the Blind / Perkins Products
Type: Company profile
Founded: 1829 (Perkins School for the Blind)

History:
Perkins introduced David Abraham's Perkins Brailler in 1951 and the SMART Brailler in 2012. Around 2010 Perkins Products also sold a Seika-made 80-cell display.

Products:
Perkins Brailler (Embossers, 1951 to present)
Perkins 80-cell display (Seika) (Braille Displays, 2010)
SMART Brailler (Embossers, 2012)
