History:
Index released the V5 line in 2016. The Basic-D V5 has a white case and black lid, embosses 140 characters per second double-sided with a dot height of 0.58 mm, and has a control panel labeled in braille and print. V5 added Index-direct-Braille (printing doc, docx, PDF and EPUB 3 files without a braille editor), printing from phones and tablets, Wi-Fi, wired network or USB connections, printing from a USB stick with speech guidance, and the BrailleApp web interface. The BrailleBox V5 cut the number of circuit boards from 4 to 2, replaced the mechanical door with a DOOR key and moved all controls to its panel. The BrailleBox has won design awards including Red Dot, Good Design and the German Design Award.
Predecessors: Index V4 platform (Index Braille, 2011)
