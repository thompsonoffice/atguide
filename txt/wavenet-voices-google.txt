WaveNet voices (Google)
Company: Google
Category: TTS Voices
Released: October 4, 2017 (Google Assistant)

Overview:
DeepMind's neural model that generates raw audio waveforms, used for the Google Assistant's US English and Japanese voices from October 2017 and in Google Cloud Text-to-Speech.

Specs:
Maker: Google

History:
The production version generated 24,000 samples a second at 16-bit, 1,000 times faster than the original research model. In listening tests the new US English voice scored a mean opinion score of 4.347, against 4.667 for human speech. Google had bought DeepMind in 2014.  DeepMind, the London AI lab, first described WaveNet in a research paper in September 2016. Instead of joining recorded fragments of speech, it builds the audio waveform one sample at a time with a neural network trained on real speech. The original model, trained on English and Mandarin, produced 16,000 samples a second and could even reproduce breaths and lip smacks.
Predecessors: Google Text-to-Speech (Speech Services by Google) (Google, October 2013 on Google Play (reported November 6, 2013))
