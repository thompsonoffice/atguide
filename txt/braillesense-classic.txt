BrailleSense (Classic)
Company: HIMS / Selvas BLV
Category: BrailleSense
Released: 2004

Overview:
The first BrailleSense (the renamed Braille Hansone), a 32-cell notetaker running Windows CE.NET.

Specs:
Released: 2004
Processor: Intel PXA255
Memory (RAM): 64 MB
Storage: 64 MB flash
Operating system: Windows CE.NET
Default speech synthesizer: Onboard speech engine (name not documented)
Battery: Detachable lithium-ion
Display or braille cells: 32 cells with 32 routing buttons; LCD
Price at launch: $4,950 (January 2005)

Physical Description:
9 x 5 x 1.5 in, 2.7 lb. Eight-key braille keyboard (dot 7 is Backspace, dot 8 is Enter) with F1 to F4. The front edge has microphone and headphone jacks and Previous, Record, Stop, Play and Next buttons.

Features:
32-cell display with 32 routing buttons
LCD screen
Two stereo speakers
Voice recorder and DAISY player controls on the front edge
Up to seven open applications
Detachable lithium-ion battery
HBL braille document format; no spell checker at review time

History:
GW Micro distributed it in the US. AccessWorld (January 2005) found it lighter than the BrailleNote and PAC Mate.
Predecessors: Braille Hansone (HIMS / Selvas BLV, 2002)
Successors: BrailleSense Plus (HIMS / Selvas BLV, 2008); BrailleSense LX (HIMS / Selvas BLV, 2008)

Ports:
2 CompactFlash slots, infrared, 2 USB, LAN, parallel, VGA video out, serial, microphone and headphone jacks

Operating System:
Windows CE.NET
Operating system history: Windows CE / Windows Mobile (on notetakers and PDAs)
Operating system history: HIMS BrailleSense software

System Requirements:
Not documented
