Detailed Description:
The Braillo 440 SF was the final step in the Braillo 270, 400S, 400 SR and 440 SW line. Braillo describes it as the world's first, and at the time only, fully automated braille book production system.
Braillo's description of its workflow: send a file and pick up a finished, folded, covered and stapled book. That made on-demand braille publishing possible.
Its predecessor, the 440 SW, introduced four-page-per-sheet embossing for simpler book production. The SF added the automatic folding, covering and stapling that came after embossing.
After American Thermoform bought Braillo in 2016, the SF line was succeeded by the Braillo 650 SF2. Mechanical and electrical upgrades raised its speed by about 50 percent.
The 440 SF's size, weight and controls could not be verified. A Russian dealer's brochure that search summaries cite for them (1,500 × 1,027 × 4,060 mm; 570 kg) returned a 404 error.
