BAUM Retec
Type: Company profile
Founded: 1980
Products listed: 4

History:
German maker of the VarioUltra and Pronto! notetakers and the COBRA and Blindows screen readers. Its hardware platform was the basis for the BrailleNote PK. It went bankrupt in 2017, and VisioBraille took over the line.

Products:
Blindows (Screen Readers, 1997)
COBRA (Screen Readers, November 2008 (version 8))
BAUM Pronto! 18 V3 (Notetakers, 2014)
BAUM VarioUltra (Braille Displays, 2014)

Operating System and Software Histories:
