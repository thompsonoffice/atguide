History:
Pocket Hal was a full screen reader for Windows Mobile PDAs, based on the desktop Hal. Dolphin showed it at Sight Village in July 2005. In October 2005 it was in final beta, with release planned for the next month. It gave speech and braille access to Pocket Word, Pocket Excel, Pocket Outlook and Pocket Internet Explorer, plus MSN and phone features. Dolphin said it would be the first screen reader for off-the-shelf PDAs to support Bluetooth or plug-in braille devices and keyboards.
