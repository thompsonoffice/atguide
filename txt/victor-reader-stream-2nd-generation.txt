Detailed Description:
The second-generation Victor Reader Stream (2014) is about 28% smaller than the first. It measures 4.5 × 2.4 × 0.7 inches and weighs 3.9 ounces with the battery, keeping the same keypad spacing.
The top edge has the SD card slot in the center and a protruding headphone jack on the right. The right side has a recessed microphone jack and, below it, a Record key with a barely detectable dot.
Across the top of the front are three keys: a square Go To key with a raised center (with the internal microphone hole above it), a round smooth Wireless key with an LED beside it, and a diamond-shaped Bookmark key.
Below them is a phone-style keypad; the 5 key has two raised dots. A raised line separates the keypad from an oval Sleep key and a bottom row of a left-arrow Rewind, a square Play/Stop and a right-arrow Fast Forward.
AccessWorld puts the round Power key and the up and down arrow keys near the top of the right edge, though the same review elsewhere says left; check the unit. Most of the back is a battery cover with a release near the top.
