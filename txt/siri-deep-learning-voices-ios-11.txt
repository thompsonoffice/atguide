History:
Apple's Machine Learning Journal (August 6, 2017) says Siri voices have been based on deep learning since iOS 10. iOS 11 used on-device deep mixture density networks to guide hybrid unit selection, from a new US English voice talent recorded for over 20 hours. The system was presented at Interspeech 2017.
Predecessors: Siri original voices (Apple, October 14, 2011 (iPhone 4S))
Successors: Siri voices (iOS 14.5) (Apple, April 2021 (iOS 14.5))
