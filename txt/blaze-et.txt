Blaze ET
Company: HIMS / Selvas BLV
Category: Book Readers

Overview:
A HIMS book player in the Blaze line. HIMS lists the Blaze ET (2015) under 'DAISY Player and OCR Multi Player' in its product history, after the Blaze EZ (2014) and before the SensePlayer OCR (2023).

Detailed Description:
The Blaze ET is a pocket book player and reader from HIMS, close to the size of a mobile phone: 116 by 58 by 16 millimeters, and 136 grams with its battery. It has no screen; small lights show battery, Wi-Fi and Bluetooth status.
At the top of the front are grilles over the stereo speakers, with the Power button between them. A long press of about four seconds turns it on or off, and a short press gives the date and time. Below are three keys, left to right: Info, Home and OCR.
In the middle is a five-key pad of four arrows around an OK key. Just below are three keys: Cancel, Menu and Review, which repeats the last announcement. The lower part of the front is a number keypad. A long press of the star key starts a key describer.
The left side has, from top to bottom, a voice-control key that chooses what to adjust, plus and minus keys, and the SD card slot. The right side has the Record key, a key-lock slider and a reset hole. The bottom edge has, from left to right, a 3.5 mm headphone jack, a micro-USB port for charging and file transfer, and a 3.5 mm jack for an external microphone or line-in.
The top edge has the lanyard attachment. On the back, the camera lens is in the middle near the top, and the battery compartment covers about three quarters of the back.

History:
Made by the Korean company HIMS in partnership with Visiole. The French CERTAM evaluation (published December 16, 2016) gives a French release date of February 2016. It differs from the Blaze EZ mainly by its number keypad, which adds navigation and text entry. It has 28 keys in all. Features include DAISY and media playback, podcasts and web radio, recording in MP3, WAV or DAISY, an FM radio, OCR with the camera, color detection, a calculator and alarms.
Predecessors: Blaze EZ (HIMS / Selvas BLV, 2014)
