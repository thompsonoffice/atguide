Blaze ET
Company: HIMS / Selvas BLV
Category: Book Readers

Overview:
A HIMS book player in the Blaze line. HIMS lists the Blaze ET (2015) under 'DAISY Player and OCR Multi Player' in its product history, after the Blaze EZ (2014) and before the SensePlayer OCR (2023).
