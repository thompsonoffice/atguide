Dolphin Computer Access
Type: Company profile
Founded: Not documented
Products listed: 6

History:
A UK maker of the Hal screen reader (DOS, then Windows), Supernova, Pocket Hal and Smart Hal, the Apollo hardware synthesizer and the Orpheus speech engine.

Hal for Windows (Screen Readers, By 1997 (Hal 1.04 for Windows 95))
Dolphin Supernova (Screen Readers, Version 8.01 in 2007 (earlier versions not documented))
Dolphin Apollo (Speech Synthesizers, Not documented)
Dolphin Orpheus (TTS Voices, Not documented)
Hal for DOS (Screen Readers, DOS era (year not documented))
Pocket Hal / Smart Hal (Screen Readers, Mid-2000s (year not documented))
