Eloquent Technology, Inc. (ETI)
Type: Company profile
Founded: 1983
Products listed: 9

History:
Founded in Ithaca, New York by Dr. Susan R. Hertz, who also taught linguistics at Cornell. It built the ETI-Eloquence formant synthesizer on her Delta system and was sold to SpeechWorks in January 2001.
