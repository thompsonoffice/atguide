MacinTalk 2
Company: Apple
Category: TTS Voices
Released: 1992 to 1994 (v1.2)

Overview:
A moderate-quality engine for low-end Macs (minimum a Mac Plus running System 6.0.7). Its ten voices were Ben, Boris, Brenda, Mariel, Marvin, Mr. Hughes, Otis, RoboVox, Votron and Xero.
