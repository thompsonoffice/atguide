Detailed Description:
The PENfriend 3 is a talking pen for labelling things around the home. You stick a coded label on an item, touch the pen tip to it and record a message. Touch the label again later and the pen plays your recording back.
It is a slim black pen, 15 cm long, 2.5 cm wide and 2 cm deep, with a 1 cm tip. It weighs 54 grams without its lanyard and has four tactile yellow buttons.
Each label holds up to an hour of audio, and labels can be re-recorded. The 16 GB internal memory holds about 250 hours of recordings, and an SD card adds up to 32 GB. Recordings can be backed up and restored with a computer.
In the box are 21 rectangular tactile labels, 84 small round orange and yellow labels, a key fob with four recording spots and a lanyard. RNIB suggests uses such as freezer food with use-by dates and cooking instructions, clothing colors and washing care, medicines, paperwork and seed packets.
It doubles as an MP3 music player and talking-book player. RNIB lists it at £114 including VAT, and eligible buyers can get VAT relief.
