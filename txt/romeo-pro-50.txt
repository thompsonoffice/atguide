Detailed Description:
The Romeo Pro 50 is the faster sibling of the Romeo 25, sold by Enabling Technologies for schools, small offices and personal use. It has the same rugged case: 21.25 inches wide, 13.25 inches deep and 8.25 inches high (6 inches without the lid), weighing 32 lb (14.5 kg).
It embosses single-sided braille at 50 characters per second on lines of up to 40 characters, with 6- or 8-dot braille and graphics.
Paper is continuous tractor-feed paper with sprocket holes, pulled through by sprocket wheels. Unlike the Romeo 25, it also takes hand-fed single sheets.
Its connections and menus follow the Romeo 25: parallel and serial ports on the back and a front control panel.
Its launch price wasn't found; Enabling Technologies' price list effective July 1, 2012 shows it at $2,995, including ET Speaks and single-sheet tractors.

History:
The Romeo Pro 50 was the faster, talking sibling of the Romeo 25 in Enabling Technologies' Romeo family. The company's November 2010 user's manual lists it at 50 characters per second with a 40-cell line, on paper up to 13.25 inches wide, in a 32-pound plastic carrying case with a handle. It adds ET Speaks speech feedback, with six voices, and a single-sheet mode for loose paper. It has parallel, serial and USB ports, a 16-key keypad, 16 stored setting menus and an 18,000-character Multi-Copy buffer that can make up to 99 copies.
