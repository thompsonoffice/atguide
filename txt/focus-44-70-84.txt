History:
Freedom Scientific's first Focus line came out in 2004 in 44-, 70- and 84-cell sizes. A second generation offered 40 and 80 cells, and Focus Blue added Bluetooth. The NFB timeline calls Focus one of the most widely used displays among JAWS users.
Successors: Focus 40 Blue (5th generation) (Freedom Scientific, 2019)
