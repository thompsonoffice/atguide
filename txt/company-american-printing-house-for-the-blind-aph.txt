American Printing House for the Blind (APH)
Type: Company profile
Founded: 1858
Products listed: 8

History:
A Louisville, Kentucky nonprofit that publishes accessible materials and products. Its electronics include the Book Port, the Braille+ Mobile Manager (with LevelStar), the Refreshabraille 18 and the Orbit Reader 20. With HumanWare it makes the Mantis Q40, the Chameleon 20 and the Monarch.

Products:
Book Port (Book Readers, May 2003)
APH Braille+ Mobile Manager (PDAs, May 2007 to 2012)
Refreshabraille 18 (Braille Displays, 2010)
Braille Plus 18 (PDAs, 2012 to February 2015 (discontinued))
Chameleon 20 (Braille Displays, 2020)
Mantis Q40 (Braille Displays, Shipping 2020)
Monarch (Braille Displays, 2024)
Book Port Plus (Book Readers, Not documented)
