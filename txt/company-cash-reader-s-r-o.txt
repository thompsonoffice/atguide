Cash Reader s.r.o.
Type: Company profile
Founded: 2018, Brno, Czech Republic
Products listed: 1

History:
A Czech assistive-technology team led by founder and CEO Tomas Jelinek (iOS), with co-founder Martin Doudera (Android). It began in 2018 because no app could recognize the Czech koruna. Its recognition technology is also licensed to smart glasses, specialized phones and digital magnifiers. Awards include the Zero Project Award 2022.
