eSpeak / eSpeak NG project
Type: Company profile
Founded: 1995
Products listed: 2

History:
Jonathan Duddington wrote 'speak' for Acorn RISC OS starting in 1995 and rewrote it as eSpeak in 2007. Reece H. Dunn maintained a fork from 2010, which officially became eSpeak NG in late 2015.

Products:
eSpeak (TTS Voices, 2007 (origins 1995))
eSpeak NG (TTS Voices, Late 2015 to present)
