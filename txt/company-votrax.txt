Votrax
Type: Company profile
Founded: 1971
Products listed: 3

History:
A Federal Screw Works division that made the Votrax phoneme synthesizers, the SC-01 chip, the Type 'N Talk (1980) and the Personal Speech System (1982). A Votrax synthesizer voiced the first Kurzweil Reading Machine.

Products:
Votrax SC-01 phoneme voice (TTS Voices, 1980s)
Votrax Type 'N Talk (Speech Synthesizers, 1980)
Votrax Personal Speech System (Speech Synthesizers, 1982)
