Vicki (Apple)
Company: Apple
Category: TTS Voices
Released: Mac OS X 10.3 Panther (2003)

Overview:
A US English female voice listed among Lion's English voices.

Specs:
Maker: Apple
First released: Mac OS X 10.3 Panther (2003)
Synthesis type: Apple does not describe the technique. AppleInsider says Vicki was added in Mac OS X Panther (2003), before Alex (10.5) and the Nuance voices of Lion.
Platforms: Mac OS X
Default on or shipped with: Not the system default (Alex is the default from Mac OS X 10.5)
Price: Not documented
Shipped with or used by: Mac OS X Speech Synthesis (MacinTalk in OS X)

Physical Description:
Not applicable (software)

Features:
Not documented

History:
A user's guide to Mac OS X speech listed Vicki among the standard system voices alongside Alex, Fred and Bruce. Vicki came from Mac OS X 10.3 (Panther, 2003): Apple greatly improved the Victoria voice and shipped the result as Vicki, keeping Victoria. Its higher-quality diphone samples made it almost 20 times larger. iOS 16 (2022) added Vicki to VoiceOver in U.S. English.
