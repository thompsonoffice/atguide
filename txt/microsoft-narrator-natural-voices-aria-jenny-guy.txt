Microsoft Narrator natural voices (Aria, Jenny, Guy)
Company: Microsoft
Category: TTS Voices
Released: January 27, 2022 (Windows 11 Insider Preview Build 22543); general release in Windows 11 version 22H2

Overview:
Microsoft's neural ('natural') voices for Narrator. They are modern on-device text-to-speech voices that work offline once downloaded.

History:
Microsoft announced the voices in Windows 11 Insider Preview Build 22543 on January 27, 2022, starting with two US English female voices, Aria and Jenny. A male US English voice, Guy, was added later, and all three are available in Windows 11 version 22H2 and later. Each is installed separately from Narrator Settings > Add natural voices. They follow Microsoft's earlier voices: Sam, Mike and Mary (SAPI 4/5 era), Anna (Vista and 7), and David, Zira, Mark, Hazel and George (Windows 8 and 10).
