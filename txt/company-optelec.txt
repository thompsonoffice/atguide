Optelec
Type: Company profile
Founded: 1975
Products listed: 3

History:
A Dutch low-vision company founded by Frans Tieman. Products: ClearView desktop magnifiers, Compact handhelds and the Traveller HD. It merged with Freedom Scientific into VFO in 2015.

Products:
Optelec Compact+ (Magnifiers, 2007)
Optelec ClearView C (Magnifiers, 2014)
Optelec Traveller HD (Magnifiers, 2015)
