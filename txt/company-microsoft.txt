Microsoft
Type: Company profile

History:
Narrator shipped with Windows 2000 on February 17, 2000. Default voices: Microsoft Sam (Windows 2000 and XP), Anna (Vista and 7), and David, Zira and Hazel (Windows 8). Narrator gained braille support in 2017.
