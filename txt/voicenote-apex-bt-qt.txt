Detailed Description:
The VoiceNote Apex is the speech-only Apex, introduced in July 2010. It is physically the same as the BrailleNote Apex but has no braille display, with an eight-dot braille keyboard (BT) or a QWERTY keyboard (QT).
A dealer spec sheet gives 612 g (1.35 lb) and 244 × 143 × 19 mm (9.61 × 5.63 × 0.78 in), a thinner case than the older VoiceNotes.
It has stereo speakers, an omnidirectional internal microphone, a user-replaceable lithium-ion battery and jacks for an external speaker or headphones and a microphone. A real-time clock keeps time for 30 days on backup power.
Ports include one SD/SDHC slot, a 10/100 Ethernet port, a USB 2.0 client port for ActiveSync and three USB 2.0 host ports. Wi-Fi (802.11b/g) and Bluetooth 2.1 are built in. A USB voice modem and a Bluetooth GPS receiver were optional.
Inside are a Freescale i.MX31 processor, 256 MB of SDRAM and 8 GB of storage, running Windows CE 6 with KeySoft. A braille terminal mode lets it work with a computer's screen reader.
