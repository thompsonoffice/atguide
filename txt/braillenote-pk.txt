BrailleNote PK
Company: Pulse Data International
Category: Notetakers
Released: Announced July 2004

Overview:
A compact 18-cell BrailleNote based on BAUM's Pronto hardware.

Specs:
Released: Announced July 2004
Processor: ARM (model not documented)
Memory (RAM): 40 MB
Storage: 16 MB flash
Operating system: Windows CE with KeySoft
Default speech synthesizer: Keynote Gold
Battery: Not documented
Display or braille cells: 18 cells
Price at launch: $4,995 (2005)

Physical Description:
About 7 x 4 x 2 in, about 1 lb.

Features:
Bluetooth
CompactFlash (Wi-Fi through a CF card)
Joystick navigation

History:
Not documented
Predecessors: BrailleNote Classic (BT/QT) (Pulse Data International, April 2000 (shipping from June 2000))

Ports:
USB, Bluetooth, CompactFlash, serial; no modem, infrared or parallel port

Operating System:
Windows CE with KeySoft
Operating system history: KeySoft
Operating system history: Windows CE / Windows Mobile (on notetakers and PDAs)

System Requirements:
Not documented
