Detailed Description:
The BrailleSense 6 (2021) measures 9.64 × 5.66 × 0.86 inches (245 × 144 × 22 mm) and weighs 1.55 pounds (705 g).
Its top panel has a nine-key Perkins-style keyboard and four scroll keys.
Nearest you is the 32-cell braille display with 32 routing keys.
The top also has a small LCD, two status lights and stereo speakers.
The front panel has a key-lock switch, a media-mode switch, five media buttons and the power button.
