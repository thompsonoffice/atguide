Victoria (Apple)
Company: Apple
Category: TTS Voices
Released: MacinTalk Pro (1993/1994) to Mac OS X

Overview:
A female MacinTalk Pro voice. In Mac OS X, Macworld calls her the female equivalent of Alex.

Specs:
Maker: Apple
First released: MacinTalk Pro (1993/1994) to Mac OS X
Synthesis type: Concatenative (MacinTalk Pro)
Platforms: System 7 to Mac OS X
Default on or shipped with: Not the system default (Alex is the default from Mac OS X 10.5)
Price: Not documented
Shipped with or used by: MacinTalk Pro

Physical Description:
Not applicable (software)

Features:
Not documented

History:
Original MacinTalk Pro voice, still a default voice in Snow Leopard and Lion. Lion included a recreation of the 1984 introduction speech in Victoria's voice. Macworld called Victoria 'the female equivalent of Alex' and one of the six voices shown by default in Snow Leopard and Lion.
