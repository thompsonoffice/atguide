ALVA BV
Type: Company profile
Founded: February 1984 (Netherlands)
Products listed: 4

History:
A Dutch braille display maker that held a cursor-routing patent. Products include the ABT-3, Satellite 544, BC640/680 and the MPO 5500 phone/PDA (2003). Optelec acquired it in September 2005.

Products:
ALVA ABT-3 series (Braille Displays, By 1994 (reviewed in the NFB's 1994 Braille Technology Evaluation; exact release year not found))
ALVA MPO 5500 (PDAs, 2003)
ALVA Satellite 544 (Braille Displays, By 2003 (in use May 2003))
ALVA BC640 / BC680 (Braille Displays, By 2007 (Optelec's BC640/BC680 user manual is copyright 2007; a user blog reviewed the BC640 in April 2010))

Operating System and Software Histories:
Not documented
