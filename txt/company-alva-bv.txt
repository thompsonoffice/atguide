ALVA BV
Type: Company profile
Founded: February 1984 (Netherlands)
Products listed: 4

History:
A Dutch braille display maker that held a cursor-routing patent. Products include the ABT-3, Satellite 544, BC640/680 and the MPO 5500 phone/PDA (2003). Optelec acquired it in September 2005.

Products:
ALVA MPO 5500 (PDAs, 2003)
ALVA Satellite 544 (Braille Displays, By 2003 (in use May 2003))
ALVA BC640 / BC680 (Braille Displays, About 2010)
ALVA ABT-3 series (Braille Displays, No release year was found in the NFB's 2026 Braille technology timeline or in ALVA material. For context, ALVA's cursor-routing patent was filed in the Netherlands in 1987 and granted in the US in 1991 (NFB timeline).)
