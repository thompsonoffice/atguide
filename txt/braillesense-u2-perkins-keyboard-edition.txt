BrailleSense U2 (Perkins-keyboard edition)
Company: HIMS / Selvas BLV
Category: BrailleSense
Released: July 2012

Overview:
The flagship Windows CE BrailleSense, with an 8-dot Perkins-style keyboard. French distributors sold it as the BrailleSense U2 Perkins.

Specs:
Released: July 2012
Processor: 1 GHz mobile CPU
Memory (RAM): Not documented
Storage: 32 GB flash

Default speech synthesizer: Eloquence
Battery: Removable lithium, about 17 hours
Display or braille cells: 32 cells plus LCD
Speech synthesizers available: Eloquence (French product sheet)

Voices: ETI-Eloquence (engine)

Physical Description:
25 x 13 x 3.9 cm, 924 g.

Detailed Description:
The BrailleSense U2 (Perkins-keyboard edition) has the shape of a small, thin rectangular box with rubber feet underneath. HIMS's manual gives 10 × 6.1 × 1.9 inches; a product sheet lists 250 × 130 × 39 mm and 924 g (about 2 pounds).
On top is a nine-key Perkins-style keyboard: dots 1 to 6, Backspace under the left little finger, Enter under the right little finger, and a long space bar under the thumbs. On the same row as the space bar are four rectangular function keys, F1 and F2 on the left, F3 and F4 on the right.
Below the keyboard is a 32-cell braille display, with 32 small oval routing buttons above it. Two capsule-shaped Up and Down scroll keys sit on each side of the display. Near the top center is an LCD with four status lights (Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, GPS and charging), and stereo speakers are in the top left and right corners.
The front edge has a microphone or line-in jack, a headphone jack, a three-position media switch (FM radio, DAISY or MP3), five media buttons and the power button. The left side has two USB ports and an SD slot; the right side has a mini USB port, the AC socket and a three-position lock switch.
The back has a reset button, an Ethernet jack, a third USB port and a 15-pin VGA output. Inside are a 1 GHz processor, 32 GB of flash and a removable lithium battery rated at about 17 hours.
