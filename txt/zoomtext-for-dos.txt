History:
Ai Squared, of Manchester Center, Vermont, began making screen and text access products in 1987 and grew from a one-man operation. AccessWorld called ZoomText the first screen magnification program on the market, in 1988. The first Windows version followed in 1991. DOS support lived on as a bundle: in AccessWorld's reviews of around 2000 and 2001 the 'Plus' levels of ZoomText Xtra included ZoomText for DOS, at $595 (magnifier) or $795 (with speech), $200 more than the Windows-only levels. Ai Squared later merged with GW Micro, and Freedom Scientific acquired it in 2016.
Successors: ZoomText (Windows) / Fusion (Freedom Scientific, 1991 to present)
