History:
KNFB Reader Mobile ported the K-NFB Reader's software from the original PDA-and-camera unit to the Nokia N82 camera phone running Symbian, at a new price of $1,595. In 2014 the NFB and Sensotec NV released an iOS app for $99, followed shortly by an Android version, which can read documents in more than thirty languages.
Predecessors: K-NFB Reader (K-NFB Reading Technology, Released July 1, 2006)
