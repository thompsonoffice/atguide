Physical Description:
170 g glasses-style device with a neck battery pack.

Detailed Description:
The eSight Go is shaped like glasses rather than a headset. The glasses part weighs about 170 g.
Inside are two 1920 × 1080 OLED screens with a 45-degree field of view.
An autofocus camera at the front gives up to 24× zoom, with adjustable contrast and color filters.
Instead of a head strap, a battery pack hangs around your neck; a neck lanyard spreads the weight. Batteries in the device and the neck pack give about 3 hours (180 minutes) per charge.
Its current list price is $4,950.
