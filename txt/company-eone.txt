Eone
Type: Company profile
Founded: 2013 (Kickstarter launch of its first watch)
Products listed: 1

History:
Eone ('designed for everyone') was founded by Hyungsoo Kim to make the Bradley tactile watch, named for Paralympic swimmer Bradley Snyder. Kim had put in $150,000 of his own money before the 2013 Kickstarter.

Products:
Bradley tactile watch (Miscellaneous, Kickstarter mid-2013; first batch planned for November 2013)
