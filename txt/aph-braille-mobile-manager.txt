Detailed Description:
The APH Braille+ Mobile Manager, made by LevelStar for APH, is a pocket-sized device about 5⅛ inches tall, 3¼ inches wide and 1 inch deep, weighing 7.4 ounces. Its gray plastic case has a brushed aluminum faceplate.
The face looks like a telephone. At the bottom is a 12-key rubber telephone keypad with a nib on the 5. Above it are OK, Menu and Cancel (Cancel is also the power key). Higher up is a four-way navigation pad with a Select button, with Status and Help keys beside it, Program 1 and Program 2 keys at the sides, and a long Applications key between them.
Flanking the rubber keys are nine blue plastic braille keys turned 90 degrees: six dot keys on one side and Shift, Space and Control on the other. To type braille you turn the unit a quarter turn clockwise, so the braille keys are farthest from you and Shift, Space and Control are nearest.
The left side has the volume key, a speaker toggle and a Record button. At the top are a phone-style earpiece in the middle and small stereo speakers on each side. The top edge has line-in, headphone and headset jacks and a mini SD slot. The bottom edge has the keypad lock switch and the power/USB connector.
It came with a 40 GB hard drive, a black nylon case with a belt loop, a wrist strap, earbuds, a USB cable, a charger and a quick-start guide in print and braille. An optional dock added a QWERTY keyboard and Ethernet.

History:
Available in May 2007 (catalog 1-07450-00, $1,395) and discontinued in 2012. Its successor, the Braille Plus 18, added a cell phone, camera, GPS and braille display and was discontinued in February 2015.
