APH Braille+ Mobile Manager
Company: American Printing House for the Blind (APH)
Category: PDAs
Released: May 2007 to 2012

Overview:
APH's version of the LevelStar Icon, with a six-key braille keyboard in place of the phone keypad.

Specs:
Released: May 2007 to 2012
Processor: Intel XScale PXA270 at 520 MHz
Memory (RAM): 64 MB
Storage: 30 GB hard drive
Operating system: Linux
Default speech synthesizer: Not documented
Battery: Not documented
Display or braille cells: Not documented
Price at launch: $1,395

Physical Description:
Not documented

Features:
RSS, podcasts, newsstand, DAISY, email
Linux console apps

History:
Available in May 2007 (catalog 1-07450-00, $1,395) and discontinued in 2012. Its successor, the Braille Plus 18, added a cell phone, camera, GPS and braille display and was discontinued in February 2015.

Ports:
USB, Bluetooth, Wi-Fi

Linux
Operating system history: Linux (on blindness devices)
