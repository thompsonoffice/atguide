Detailed Description:
The Braille 'n Speak Scholar is a student version of Blazie's Braille 'n Speak pocket notetaker, sold through the American Printing House for the Blind (APH) in partnership with Freedom Scientific. APH recommended it for ages 8 and up and described it as an entry-level notetaker, with a simpler interface for first-time users.
Like the Braille 'n Speak it is based on, the Scholar has no braille display. You type on a seven-key braille-style keyboard: six dot keys plus a space bar, laid out like a Perkins braillewriter. Everything is spoken back through a built-in speaker or an earphone. After the 2004 upgrade the voice was DoubleTalk speech.
Built-in tools include a word processor with file organizing, a braille-to-print back translator for printing in braille or print, and a talking clock. There is also a countdown timer, a stopwatch, a phone directory, a calendar, an appointment book and a scientific graphing calculator.
It connects to a PC through a serial cable. The cable has a 9-pin female plug at the PC end and an 8-pin mini-DIN plug at the Scholar end, so the port on the unit is a small round mini-DIN socket. APH sold parallel-printer and ImageWriter cables separately.
In the box were a soft leather carrying case, a battery charger/AC adapter, an earphone, the serial cable, braille and print reference manuals, a cassette tutorial with demos, a longer manual on CD-ROM, WinDisc and WinPrint software and a one-year warranty. APH product photos show students seated at a table, with their hands on the seven-key keyboard or listening through earphones.

History:
APH listed it from spring 2000 until 2004 (APH catalog number 1-07309-01). In September 2004 APH sent its entire stock back to Freedom Scientific to upgrade it with higher-quality DoubleTalk speech, better PC formatting and printing and a better appointment book. The upgraded model got the new catalog number 1-07320-01 and a $1,140 price, and existing owners were offered an upgrade for about $425. In October 2006 APH cut the price from $1,249 to $800 for the next 400 units ordered.
