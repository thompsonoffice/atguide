History:
One of DECtalk's nine built-in voices. In the DECtalk Software reference (2001, Force Computers), Rita is described as the 'female voice' and is chosen with the in-line command [:name RITA] or [:nr]. The Speak applet gives it local names: Rita in Spanish, Rita in German and Jacqueline in French. Its stock settings include an average pitch of 106 Hz, a head size of 95%, breathiness of 46 dB and a pitch range of 80%. Any voice can be reshaped with the Design Voice [:dv] command, and changes can be saved as a tenth voice, Val.
