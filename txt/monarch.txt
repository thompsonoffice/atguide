Monarch
Company: American Printing House for the Blind (APH)
Category: Braille Displays
Released: 2024

Overview:
An APH/HumanWare dynamic tactile device with 10 lines of 32 cells for braille and graphics, using Dot cells. Named one of TIME's Best Inventions of 2025.
