Vinux
Company: Linux accessibility community
Category: Accessible Linux
Released: 2008 to January 18, 2017 (discontinued)

Overview:
An Ubuntu remaster for blind and visually impaired users by Tony Sales of RNC Hereford. It began as Vibuntu and was renamed Vinux in January 2009.

Specs:
Developer: Linux accessibility community
Release years: 2008 to January 18, 2017 (discontinued)
Runs on: Ubuntu-based
Default speech: Not documented
Price: Not documented

Physical Description:
Not applicable (software)

Features:
Orca screen reader
Speakup console speech
Compiz magnifier
USB braille display support

History:
Releases include 1.4 (March 2009), 1.5 (May 2009), 2.0 (September 2009), 3.0 (listed on DistroWatch on June 1, 2010) and 5.1 (January 18, 2017), the last.
