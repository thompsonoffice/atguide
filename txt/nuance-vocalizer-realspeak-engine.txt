Nuance Vocalizer / RealSpeak (engine)
Company: Nuance Communications
Category: TTS Voices
Released: Mid-2000s to present. Nuance built its TTS line through acquisitions, including Rhetorical (2004) and SVOX (2011), and Vocalizer was the TTS in the first-generation Victor Reader Stream (2007). The first release date of the Vocalizer brand itself was not found.

Overview:
Nuance's concatenative TTS family. Voices include Tom, Samantha, Daniel and Karen, according to a fan-maintained list that should be treated as a weak source.
