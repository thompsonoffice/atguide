History:
Index's history of the Basic-D says the Basic Blue Bar was developed in 1988 as part of its first generation of modern braille embossers. It took tractor-fed paper. A UK service note describes the V1 Basic by the blue or red bar on its steel case; V1 production ran until 1995, and no spare parts remain.
Predecessors: Index Computer Braille Printer (Index 3.7) (Index Braille, 1984)
Successors: Index Everest-D (V1) (Index Braille, 1992)
