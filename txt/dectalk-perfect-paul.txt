DECtalk Perfect Paul
Company: Digital Equipment Corporation (DEC)
Category: TTS Voices
Released: 1984 to present (via DECtalk software)

Overview:
The standard male DECtalk voice and the factory default.

Specs:
Maker: Digital Equipment Corporation (DEC)
First released: 1984 to present (via DECtalk software)
Synthesis type: Formant (Klatt synthesis by rule)
Platforms: DECtalk hardware (DTC01, Express), DECtalk Software for Windows NT and others
Default on or shipped with: DECtalk DTC01 (factory default)
Price: Not documented
Shipped with or used by: DECtalk DTC01; Mobile Speak; Emacspeak

Physical Description:
Not applicable (software)

Features:
Not documented

History:
Created from Dennis Klatt's MITalk/Klattalk formant synthesis-by-rule research and commercialized by DEC in 1984. Voices are selected with [:np]. DECtalk passed to Fonix, and Fonix DECtalk was used by Code Factory's Mobile Speak.
