Detailed Description:
'Braille 'n Speak Classic' is the name later given to the original 1987 Braille 'n Speak after larger-memory models arrived. Blazie Technologies' 2025 retrospective says it had 180K of storage in 45 pages of 4,096 characters each, plus a help file of no more than one page.
Physically it is the original Braille 'n Speak: a flat box about the size of a videocassette, with six braille dot keys and a space bar laid out like a Perkins braillewriter, and speech output instead of a braille display. See the Braille 'n Speak entry for its full description.
