Digital Equipment Corporation (DEC)
Type: Company profile
Founded: 1957
Products listed: 10

History:
Commercialized Dennis Klatt's MITalk/Klattalk research as DECtalk in 1984. The DECtalk intellectual property later passed to Fonix.

Products:
DECtalk Beautiful Betty (TTS Voices, 1984)
DECtalk Doctor Dennis (TTS Voices, 1984)
DECtalk DTC01 (Speech Synthesizers, 1984)
DECtalk Frail Frank (TTS Voices, 1984)
DECtalk Huge Harry (TTS Voices, 1984)
DECtalk Kit the Kid (TTS Voices, 1984)
DECtalk Perfect Paul (TTS Voices, 1984 to present (via DECtalk software))
DECtalk Rough Rita (TTS Voices, 1984)
DECtalk Uppity Ursula (TTS Voices, 1984)
DECtalk Whispering Wendy (TTS Voices, 1984)
