Google speech engines on Android
Company: Google
Category: Operating system or software version history
Versions listed: 3

Overview:
From SVOX Pico in Android 1.6 to the Google Text-to-Speech app and WaveNet neural voices.

Version History:

Version 1: SVOX Pico
Released: September 2009
Notes: Built into Android 1.6 Donut: US and UK English, French, German, Italian, Spanish.

Version 2: Google Text-to-Speech app
Released: October 2013
Notes: Standalone Play Store app.

Version 3: WaveNet
Released: October 4, 2017
Notes: Neural voices for Google Assistant US English and Japanese.

Devices and Software That Ran It:
SVOX Pico (Android) (SVOX, September 2009 (Android 1.6 Donut))
Google Text-to-Speech (Speech Services by Google) (Google, October 2013 on Google Play (reported November 6, 2013))
WaveNet voices (Google) (Google, October 4, 2017 (Google Assistant))
TalkBack (Google, October 2009 (Android 1.6 Donut) to present)

System Requirements:
Not documented
