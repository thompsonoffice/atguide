Microsoft Mark
Company: Microsoft
Category: TTS Voices
Released: 2015 (Windows 10)

Overview:
An additional US English male voice listed for Narrator in Windows 10 and 11.

Specs:
Maker: Microsoft
First released: 2015 (Windows 10)
Synthesis type: Microsoft does not publish the technique. Microsoft lists this as a standard Windows TTS voice, which Windows 11 calls a 'legacy voice', as distinct from the newer neural 'natural voices'.
Platforms: Windows 10 and 11
Default on or shipped with: Not a default; mobile voice added to the Windows 10 US English language pack (Windows Phone 8 and 10 Mobile voice)
Price: Not documented
Shipped with or used by: Microsoft Narrator

Physical Description:
Not applicable (software)

Features:
Not documented

History:
Mark and Zira are the Windows Phone 8, 8.1 and Windows 10 Mobile voices, made available on desktop Windows 10. Windows 11 reclassified them as 'legacy voices' after the Narrator natural voices arrived. With Windows 10, Microsoft tried to use the same speech voices across all its platforms. Mark is not the voice of Cortana; Cortana's organic responses used a hidden voice called Eva. Microsoft has updated the mobile voices through Windows updates to sound more natural than the originals.
