Detailed Description:
AccessWorld described Blazie's Braille Lite as identical to the Braille 'n Speak with a braille display added. The Braille Lite 40 (1996) has a 40-cell display. No source checked gives its own size and weight. For comparison, the smaller Braille Lite 18 measured 5 × 8.25 × 2 inches.
Like the Braille 'n Speak, it has a Perkins-style braille keyboard with a space bar, and commands are chords of the space bar plus dot keys.
Its 40-cell refreshable braille display has a cursor routing button above each cell, and it uses advance bars to move through text a display-length at a time.
Speech comes from a built-in DoubleTalk synthesizer through a speaker or earphones.
Its software includes a word processor, calendar, clock and calculator, with optional 8-dot and two-language braille support. It could also serve as a braille display for a computer.
