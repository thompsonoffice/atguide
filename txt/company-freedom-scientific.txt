Freedom Scientific
Type: Company profile
Founded: 2000
Products listed: 15

History:
Formed in 2000 from Henter-Joyce (JAWS), Arkenstone's reading-machine line (OpenBook) and Blazie Engineering (Braille 'n Speak). Makes JAWS, the Focus braille displays, PAC Mate and the TOPAZ and RUBY magnifiers. In late 2015 Vector Capital merged it with Optelec to form VFO, which was renamed Vispero in 2018. Freedom Scientific remains a Vispero brand.
