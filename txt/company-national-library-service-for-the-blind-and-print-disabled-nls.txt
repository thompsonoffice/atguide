National Library Service for the Blind and Print Disabled (NLS)
Type: Company profile
Founded: 1931
Products listed: 2

History:
The Library of Congress talking-book program. It issued the cartridge-based DS1/DA1 digital talking-book players in 2009 and an NLS eReader braille display in 2020.
