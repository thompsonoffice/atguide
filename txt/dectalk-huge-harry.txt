History:
One of DECtalk's nine built-in voices. In the DECtalk Software reference (2001, Force Computers), Harry is described as the 'full male voice' and is chosen with the in-line command [:name HARRY] or [:nh]. The Speak applet gives it local names: Humberto in Spanish, Hans in German and Michel in French. Its stock settings include an average pitch of 89 Hz, a head size of 115%, breathiness of 0 dB and a pitch range of 80%. Any voice can be reshaped with the Design Voice [:dv] command, and changes can be saved as a tenth voice, Val.
