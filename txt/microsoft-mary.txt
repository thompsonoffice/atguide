Microsoft Mary
Company: Microsoft
Category: TTS Voices
Released: Windows 2000 / XP era

Overview:
An optional female voice, downloadable for Windows 2000 and XP.

Specs:
Maker: Microsoft
First released: Windows 2000 / XP era
Synthesis type: Concatenative (Microsoft Research Whistler engine with unit selection and LPC vocoder)
Platforms: Windows (SAPI 4 and 5)
Default on or shipped with: None (optional download)
Price: Not documented

Physical Description:
Not applicable (software)

Features:
Not documented

History:
Distributed the same way as Microsoft Mike. Mary is Microsoft's optional female voice for Windows 2000 and XP. Its SAPI 5 version was distributed as a Merge Module and installed by apps such as Microsoft Reader; the SAPI 4 version differs in pronunciation. Michael and Michelle, licensed from Lernout & Hauspie, came with Office XP, Office 2003 and Microsoft Reader as other optional voices.
