Detailed Description:
The BrailleSense 6 Mini (2022) is the 20-cell version of the BrailleSense 6. It measures about 185 × 103 × 23 mm (7.1 × 4.1 × 0.9 in) and weighs about 430 g (0.95 lb).
The top panel has a nine-key Perkins-style keyboard, Control and Alt keys and four function keys.
Below the keyboard is the 20-cell braille display. A routing key is above each cell, with a raised dot on every fifth key, and there are four scroll keys.
The right side has two USB-C ports. The left side has volume controls and headphone and microphone jacks.
It shares the BrailleSense 6 software and Android platform.

History:
The BrailleSense 6 mini, which HIMS's product history dates to 2022, is the 20-cell companion to the 32-cell BrailleSense 6. Selvas BLV calls it a lightweight braille tablet and sells it for $3,995. It runs Android 12 on an eight-core processor with 6 GB of memory and 128 GB of storage. It has a Perkins-style keyboard, a 13-megapixel camera, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth 5.1, GPS, an FM radio and a user-replaceable 3,400 mAh battery. It measures 7.08 by 4.06 by 0.91 inches, weighs 0.95 pounds, and can send video to a monitor through USB-C.
Predecessors: BrailleSense Polaris Mini (HIMS / Selvas BLV, 2018)
