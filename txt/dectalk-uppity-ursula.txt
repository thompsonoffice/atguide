History:
One of DECtalk's nine built-in voices. In the DECtalk Software reference (2001, Force Computers), Ursula is described as the 'aged female voice' and is chosen with the in-line command [:name URSULA] or [:nu]. The Speak applet gives it local names: Úrsula in Spanish, Ursula in German and Angèle in French. Its stock settings include an average pitch of 240 Hz, a head size of 95%, breathiness of 0 dB and a pitch range of 135%. Any voice can be reshaped with the Design Voice [:dv] command, and changes can be saved as a tenth voice, Val.
