Accessible-Coconut
Company: Zendalona
Category: Accessible Linux
Released: Releases through 22.04.04 (ISO July 2025) and 24.04.01

Overview:
A community GNU/Linux distribution based on Ubuntu MATE, fully accessible without vision.

Features:
Screen reader with speech and braille
Print reading with a scanner
Six-key braille input
Typing tutor, math tutor, accessible chess
DAISY player and ebook speaker
Console (TTY) screen reader
Screen magnification

History:
Accessible-Coconut comes from the Zendalona community in Kerala, India. Its roots go back to K. Sathyaseelan, who lost his sight at age 10 in 1970 and moved to Ubuntu and the Orca screen reader in 2007. Nalin Sathyan, Zendalona's founder and a three-time Google Summer of Code winner, built tools such as Easy-OCR (2009), Lios OCR (2011), Sharada Braille Writer (2012) and IBus-Braille (accepted into Debian in 2015). The first Accessible-Coconut release came in 2014. The group took the name Zendalona in 2020, registered as a non-profit in 2022, and became a Google Summer of Code mentoring organization in 2024.
