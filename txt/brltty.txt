BRLTTY
Company: Linux accessibility community
Category: Screen Readers
Released: 1995 (0.22 beta) to present

Overview:
A background service that gives braille-display access to the Linux text console.

History:
Started in 1995 by Cambridge students Nikhil Nair and James Bowden; Nair wanted to use his CombiBraille. Version 0.22 beta was announced in December 1995 and 1.0 in July 1996. Speech support came in 1.0.1. Nicolas Pitre took over in 1998 (1.9.0), and Dave Mielke from 2001.
