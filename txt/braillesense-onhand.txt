Detailed Description:
The BrailleSense OnHand (2010) is a small 18-cell notetaker. AccessWorld measured it at 4 × 7 × 1.5 inches; the Japanese distributor Extra lists it at 17.2 × 9.0 × 2.7 cm and 425 g (about 15 oz). It has a white top panel on a black body and comes in a sturdy case with a shoulder strap that leaves every button reachable.
On top is an eight-key Perkins-style keyboard, with a row of four evenly spaced function keys below it. The small speaker is on the top panel between and above the dot 1 and dot 7 keys.
Below the function keys are 18 routing buttons over the 18 eight-dot braille cells, with two scroll keys on each side of the display.
The front edge has a three-position audio mode switch and five audio buttons. The left side has the AC socket, a USB port and a lock switch; the right side has the SD card slot and external microphone and headset jacks.
The battery door is on the bottom back edge. The battery has clear tactile guides for removing and replacing it, and a spare battery and a charger that beeps when charging is done come in the box.
