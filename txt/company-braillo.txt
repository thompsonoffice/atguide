Braillo
Type: Company profile
Founded: 1980
Products listed: 3

History:
A Norwegian company. In January 1980, working with SINTEF, it introduced the Braillo 270, the first double-sided (interpoint) embosser. American Thermoform acquired it in 2016.

Products:
Braillo 270 (Embossers, January 1980)
Braillo 200 (Embossers, 1986)
Braillo 440 SF (Embossers, Not documented)
