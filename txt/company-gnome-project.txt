GNOME Project
Type: Company profile
Founded: 1997

History:
The GNOME desktop's screen reader is Orca (2006, originally from Sun Microsystems), which replaced Gnopernicus in GNOME 2.16. IBM's Linux Screen Reader (LSR) ran from 2006 to 2007.

Gnopernicus (Screen Readers, December 7, 2001 (first release) to 2006 (1.1.2, August 21, 2006))
Orca (Screen Readers, 2006 to present)
