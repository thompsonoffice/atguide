MacinTalk Pro
Company: Apple
Category: TTS Voices
Released: September 1993 (v1.0); v1.2 February 1994

Overview:
Apple's high-quality engine, with the voices Agnes, Bruce and Victoria in High Quality and Small versions.

Specs:
Maker: Apple
First released: September 1993 (v1.0); v1.2 February 1994
Synthesis type: Concatenative
Platforms: System 7 on 68020/68040 and PowerPC Macs; about 1.5 MB of memory
Default on or shipped with: No default voice is named in the sources searched. The voices were Agnes, Bruce and Victoria, each in High Quality and Small versions.
Price: Not documented
Voices: Agnes (Apple); Bruce (Apple); Victoria (Apple)

Physical Description:
Not applicable (software)

Features:
Not documented

History:
Required a 68020 or better (later surveys say 68040) and System 7. Described as the first concatenative Macintosh voices. A Mexican Spanish edition (Carlos and Catalina) was given away with PlainTalk 1.5 in 1996.
Predecessors: MacinTalk (original) (Apple, 1984 (MacinTalk 1.1 May 1985))
