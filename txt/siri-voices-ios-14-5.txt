Siri voices (iOS 14.5)
Company: Apple
Category: TTS Voices
Released: April 2021 (iOS 14.5)

Overview:
iOS 14.5 added two American English voices. The four US voices are labeled Voice 1 to Voice 4, and Siri no longer has a default voice: users choose one at setup.
