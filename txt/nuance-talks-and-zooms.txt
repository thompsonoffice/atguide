Nuance TALKS (and ZOOMS)
Company: Nuance Communications
Category: Screen Readers
Released: By 2004 to about 2013

Overview:
A screen reader for Symbian Series 60 and Series 80 phones, mostly Nokia, using Eloquence speech.

Specs:
Developer: Nuance Communications
Release years: By 2004 to about 2013
Runs on: Symbian S60 3rd/5th Edition, Symbian^3, Series 80
Default speech: Eloquence
Price: Not documented
Voices: ETI-Eloquence (engine)

Physical Description:
Not applicable (software)

Features:
Not documented

History:
AFB tested TALKS and ZOOMS Premium Edition 3.0 in October 2006. It ran on about 20 Series 60 phones and about 5 Series 80 phones. It became obsolete when Symbian was discontinued in 2013. AccessWorld's 2004 review lists TALKS as produced by Brand & Gröber Communications and tested version 1.30.6 for Series 60 on a Nokia 6620 against Code Factory's Mobile Speak 1.0. Both used Eloquence. TALKS ran on the Nokia 3600, 3620, 3650, 3660, 6600, 6620, 7650, N-Gage and the Siemens SX1.
