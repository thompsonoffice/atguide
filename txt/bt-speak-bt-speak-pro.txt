BT Speak / BT Speak Pro
Company: Blazie Technologies
Category: Notetakers
Released: 2024 to present

Overview:
Deane Blazie's modern speech-only braille-keyboard notetaker, built on a Raspberry Pi compute module.

Specs:
Released: 2024 to present
Processor: Raspberry Pi CM4, Broadcom BCM2711 at 1.5 GHz (Pro)
Memory (RAM): 8 GB (Pro)
Storage: 32 GB (Pro)
Operating system: Linux on Raspberry Pi CM4; custom Traditional mode plus a MATE desktop with the Orca screen reader
Default speech synthesizer: Not documented
Battery: 8,200 mAh, about 10 hours
Display or braille cells: Not documented
Price at launch: $795 (BT Speak), $1,195 (Pro)

Physical Description:
Pro: 6.1 x 2.8 x 0.75 in, 240.9 g, 9-key Perkins-style keyboard.

Features:
Perkins-style braille keyboard
Speech output
Wi-Fi and Bluetooth 5.0

History:
Blazie Technologies released it in 2024 as a spiritual successor to the Braille 'n Speak.
Predecessors: Braille 'n Speak (original) (Blazie Engineering, 1987 to 1990s)

Ports:
USB-C, mini-HDMI, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth 5.0

Operating System:
Linux on Raspberry Pi CM4; custom Traditional mode plus a MATE desktop with the Orca screen reader

System Requirements:
Not documented
