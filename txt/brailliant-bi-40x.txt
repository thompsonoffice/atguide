Detailed Description:
The Brailliant BI 40X is a 40-cell braille display from HumanWare with simple built-in apps. It weighs about 690 grams.
On the top surface, from back to front, are eight braille keys for dots 1 to 8, then 40 cursor-routing buttons, then the 40 braille cells. At each end of the cells are three command keys arranged like a braille cell. In front of the cells are two spacebars, one under each thumb.
The front edge has four thumb keys for navigation, with a round Home/Select button in the center.
The left edge has, from back to front, a USB-C port for a computer or charger, the Power button, and a USB-A port for flash drives. The right edge has an earphone jack and volume up and down buttons.
The top also has built-in stereo speakers. At the 2021 review they only gave system beeps, though HumanWare planned to add audio.

History:
AccessWorld called it a new class of smart braille display. It can connect to Wi-Fi and log into NLS BARD, and its KeyPad editor is meant for quick notes: press Power and you can type within about five seconds. The reviewer missed a spellchecker. The smaller BI 20X has Bluetooth 4.0 instead of 5.0 and an SD slot instead of a USB storage port.
Predecessors: Brailliant 20 / 40 (HumanWare, 2004)
