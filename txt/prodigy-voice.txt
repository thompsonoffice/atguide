Detailed Description:
The Prodigy Voice is a blood glucose meter that speaks every step: setup, testing, results, memory, settings, warnings and errors. AccessWorld measured it at 2.2 by 4.0 by 0.8 inches and 2.4 ounces.
On the front is a 1.2 by 1.5 inch black-on-gray display. Three control buttons sit below it and two direction buttons to its right. The largest is the round power button on the right, with a raised M; it also opens Memory Recall. The middle button is a triangle with a raised S for Settings. The left one has a raised left arrow and repeats the last spoken message. The up and down buttons have raised arrows.
The speaker is below the control buttons. A 3.5 mm headphone jack near the bottom of the right side gives private listening, and below it a mini-USB port sends results to Prodigy's software. A lanyard slot is on the bottom left.
Strips need no coding and use capillary action, so a 0.6 microliter sample is drawn in. A tactile notch shows where to insert the strip. The meter speaks results in 6 or 7 seconds and warns 'Not enough blood' to help avoid false lows.
It stores 450 readings with spoken 7-, 14-, 21-, 28-, 60- and 90-day averages, and in 2012 it cost about $40. AFB Tech found its buttons among the easiest to tell apart by touch and its display the highest-contrast of the meters tested.
