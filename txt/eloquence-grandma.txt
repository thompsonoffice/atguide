History:
Part of the ETI-Eloquence voice set. Eloquence, the default speech in JAWS, came to Apple devices with iOS 16 (public release September 12, 2022), in both U.S. and U.K. English, as one of eight voices: Eddy, Flo, Grandma, Grandpa, Reed, Rocko, Sandy and Shelley. AppleVis noted that many users like Eloquence for its fast response and predictable, comparatively robotic sound, which can be easier to follow at high rates. iOS 17 added per-voice settings for Eloquence: rate multiplier, head size, pitch, pitch range, breathiness and roughness, plus a higher sampling rate, phrase prediction, an abbreviation dictionary and a community pronunciation dictionary.
