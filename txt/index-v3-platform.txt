History:
Basic-D V3 came out in 2002. A UK service note describes V3 machines as having a black case, red lid and yellow buttons, with USB, Ethernet and parallel ports (the serial port is for service only), and serial numbers from 30000 to 49999. Production ran until 2011. V3 is now end-of-life with no parts available.
Predecessors: Index Basic and Everest (V2 platform) (Index Braille, February 1995 to 2002)
Successors: Index V4 platform (Index Braille, 2011)
