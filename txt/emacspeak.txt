Emacspeak
Company: Linux accessibility community
Category: Screen Readers
Released: October 1994 (open source April 1995) to present

Overview:
T. V. Raman's complete audio desktop for Emacs. It speaks the underlying information rather than reading the visual screen.

Specs:
Developer: Linux accessibility community
Release years: October 1994 (open source April 1995) to present
Runs on: Emacs on Unix/Linux and others
Default speech: DECtalk Express originally
Price: Not documented
Voices: DECtalk Perfect Paul

Physical Description:
Not applicable (software)

Features:
Not documented

History:
Raman began it in October 1994 on a Linux laptop with a DECtalk Express, and speech-enabled the Emacs calendar in its first weeks. Its speech-server abstraction let it add IBM ViaVoice in 1999. The 2004 Speakup FAQ listed it at version 20.
