Microsoft George
Company: Microsoft
Category: TTS Voices
Released: 2015 (Windows 10 OneCore voice)

Overview:
A UK English male voice listed for Narrator.

Specs:
Maker: Microsoft
First released: 2015 (Windows 10 OneCore voice)
Synthesis type: Microsoft does not publish the technique. Microsoft lists this as a standard Windows TTS voice, which Windows 11 calls a 'legacy voice', as distinct from the newer neural 'natural voices'.
Platforms: Windows 10 and 11
Default on or shipped with: Not the default for US English (Microsoft lists David and Mark as US male voices). George is the English (Great Britain) male voice, alongside Hazel and Susan.
Price: Not documented

Not applicable (software)

Features:

History:
Windows 10 made the mobile voice packs universal: each downloaded language pack adds one male and one female voice. Windows 11 calls these legacy voices. These mobile voices came from Windows Phone 8 and 8.1 and Windows 10 Mobile. Most desktop language packs had only female voices, but every mobile pack came as a male and female pair, so downloading a language added both. Microsoft has updated the mobile voices through Windows updates to sound more natural than the originals.
