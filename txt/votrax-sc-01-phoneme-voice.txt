History:
Votrax (founded 1971, a Federal Screw Works division) produced phoneme synthesizers. The SC-01 chip was used in the Type 'N Talk (1980). Votrax began in 1971 as a division of Federal Screw Works in the Detroit area. It took its name in 1974 from its first commercial product, the VS4 'Votrax', and became the independent Votrax International in 1980. The SC-01 chip (1980) put a whole phoneme synthesizer on a single integrated circuit. The SC-01-A (1981) was used in the Type 'N Talk and the Personal Speech System, as well as arcade games such as Wizard of Wor and Gottlieb pinball machines. It was succeeded by the SC-02, made by Silicon Systems as the SSI-263P, which became the voice of Artic's SynPhonix cards. A Votrax synthesizer was also part of the first Kurzweil Reading Machine.
