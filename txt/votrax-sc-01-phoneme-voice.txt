Votrax SC-01 phoneme voice
Company: Votrax
Category: TTS Voices
Released: 1980s

The phoneme-based voice of Votrax products and the voice of the first-generation Kurzweil Reading Machine.

Votrax (founded 1971, a Federal Screw Works division) produced phoneme synthesizers. The SC-01 chip was used in the Type 'N Talk (1980).
