HumanWare
Type: Company profile
Founded: 2005 (as HumanWare Group)
Products listed: 23

History:
Originally the US distributor for Pulse Data. Pulse Data bought it in 2001, and the 2005 merger with VisuAide created the HumanWare Group (Longueuil, Quebec, and Christchurch). Product lines: BrailleNote, Victor Reader, Brailliant, explorē and Connect magnifiers, the DeafBlind Communicator and, with APH, the Mantis, Chameleon and Monarch.
