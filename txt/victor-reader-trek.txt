Detailed Description:
The Victor Reader Trek (2017) combines the second-generation Stream with the Trekker Breeze GPS. It weighs 5 ounces and measures 114 × 62 × 24 mm, resting easily in one hand. It comes in a fitted silicone case.
The face has three distinctly shaped, widely spaced keys across the top. The middle one is the round Online key that also starts GPS.
Below them is a 12-key telephone keypad: the 5 has two dots, and the 2, 4, 6 and 8 keys have raised semicircles on their edges. A boldly raised line separates the keypad from the bottom keys.
Below the line is an oval key for time, date and sleep timer, then an arrow-shaped Rewind, a rectangular Play/Stop and an arrow-shaped Fast Forward.
The top edge has the SD slot and a 3.5 mm jack; the bottom edge a micro USB port. The left edge has the power button and up and down keys for volume, speed and tone; the right edge has the Record button. The lithium-ion battery is user-replaceable.

History:
HumanWare announced the Victor Reader Trek in summer 2017. It combines the second-generation Victor Reader Stream with the GPS wayfinding of the Trekker Breeze (about 2011). Customers who ordered that summer or fall reported getting their units only in January and February 2018. AccessWorld's review listed the price as $699 US or $895 Canadian.
Predecessors: Victor Reader Stream (2nd generation) (HumanWare, February 2013); Trekker Breeze (HumanWare, 2008)
