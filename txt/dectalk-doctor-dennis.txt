History:
One of DECtalk's nine built-in voices. Its name nods to Dennis Klatt. In the DECtalk Software reference (2001, Force Computers), Dennis is described as the 'male voice' and is chosen with the in-line command [:name DENNIS] or [:nd]. The Speak applet gives it local names: Domingo in Spanish, Dieter in German and Joël in French. Its stock settings include an average pitch of 110 Hz, a head size of 105%, breathiness of 38 dB and a pitch range of 135%. Any voice can be reshaped with the Design Voice [:dv] command, and changes can be saved as a tenth voice, Val.
