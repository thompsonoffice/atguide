PAC Mate BX400 / QX400
Company: Freedom Scientific
Category: PDAs
Released: 2002 (AccessWorld); some sources say 2003

Overview:
An accessible Pocket PC running JAWS for Pocket PC, with a braille (BX) or QWERTY (QX) keyboard and optional 20- or 40-cell braille.

Specs:
Released: 2002 (AccessWorld); some sources say 2003
Processor: Intel XScale 400 MHz
Memory (RAM): 64 MB
Storage: 32 MB flash
Operating system: Windows CE.NET 4.2 / Pocket PC 2003
Default speech synthesizer: Eloquence
Battery: 4,320 mAh
Display or braille cells: Not documented
Speech synthesizers available: Not documented
Price at launch: BX/QX400 $2,395; 420 (20 cells) $3,795; 440 (40 cells) $5,595
Used price today (eBay): Not documented
Voices: ETI-Eloquence (engine)

Physical Description:
Not documented

Features:
JAWS for Pocket PC
Two CompactFlash Type II slots

History:
AccessWorld (2005) says the PAC Mate was first released in 2002. The braille display detaches: a 40-cell BX weighs about 4 lb, and under 2 lb without the display. Its software included FS Edit.
Successors: PAC Mate Omni (Freedom Scientific, Fall 2007)

Ports:
USB OTG (mini-USB), IrDA, 2 CF Type II slots, headphone and microphone jacks

Operating System:
Windows CE.NET 4.2 / Pocket PC 2003
Operating system history: Windows CE / Windows Mobile (on notetakers and PDAs)
Operating system history: JAWS versions

Not documented
