Samantha (Apple)
Company: Apple
Category: TTS Voices
Released: Mac OS X 10.7 Lion (2011) onward

Overview:
A US English female voice, downloadable in Lion. Macworld called it close to, but not exactly, Siri.

Specs:
Maker: Apple
First released: Mac OS X 10.7 Lion (2011) onward
Synthesis type: Nuance (licensed by Apple)
Platforms: Mac OS X 10.7 and later
Default on or shipped with: Not the system default (Alex is the default from Mac OS X 10.5)
Price: Not documented
Shipped with or used by: Mac OS X Speech Synthesis (MacinTalk in OS X)

Physical Description:
Not applicable (software)

Features:
Not documented

History:
Added as a free download in Lion. Snow Leopard had no Samantha. Its relationship to Nuance voices is not confirmed in these sources. AppleInsider listed Samantha in March 2011 as one of three new high-quality US English voices for Lion, with Tom and Jill. In April 2012 9to5Mac, following a Reddit user, reported that the downloadable Samantha was the same voice as Siri's in the United States; Lion asked to confirm a 469 MB download when it was selected.
