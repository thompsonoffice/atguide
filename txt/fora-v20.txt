Detailed Description:
The Fora V20 is a talking blood glucose meter from Fora Care. AccessWorld measured it at 3.69 by 2.25 by 0.78 inches and 2.08 ounces.
A 1.2 by 1.5 inch black-on-gray display sits above three buttons. The right-most is Power and Memory Recall, with a raised circle around an M. The middle is Settings, with a raised square around an S. The left repeats the last spoken message and has a raised left-pointing arrow. Up and down buttons with raised arrows are to the right of the display.
A strip-eject slider on the top right side pushes used strips out, so you never touch them. The speaker is below the Repeat and Settings buttons.
A 3.5 mm headphone jack is on the bottom left side, with a rubber-covered data port marked PC in raised letters below it. A lanyard slot is on the bottom right.
It stores 450 readings and speaks 7- to 90-day averages, with a language setting. AccessWorld found its buttons among the easiest to tell apart, but its PC software and electronic manual were not screen-reader accessible.
