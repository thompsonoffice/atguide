NeoSpeech
Type: Company profile
Founded: February 2002, as Voiceware's US subsidiary in San Jose
Products listed: 1

History:
A TTS vendor whose US English voices included Paul, Julie and Kate. It later moved its hosting to Amazon. Its Kate voice was released in September 2001 and Paul in May 2002. Voiceware and NeoSpeech joined the ReadSpeaker brand in 2019.

Products:
NeoSpeech Kate, Paul and Julie (TTS Voices, September 2001 (Kate, as Voiceware VoiceText))
