LookTel Money Reader
Company: IPPLEX (LookTel)
Category: Miscellaneous
Released: March 8, 2011 (iOS)

Overview:
An early iPhone app that named U.S. bills from $1 to $100 in real time through the camera, without an internet connection.

History:
IPPLEX released it on March 8, 2011 for $1.99, for the iPhone 4, iPhone 3GS and fourth-generation iPod touch. It used LookTel's patented object recognition on a continuous video stream, so you didn't need to hold still or take a photo, and it spoke results in a clear female voice. AccessWorld's Scott Davert called it unequivocally a top performer. In 2016 NantMobile Health bought it and moved it to a subscription; by 2020 the version he had bought in 2011 was no longer available.
