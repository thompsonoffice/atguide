History:
Kirk Reiser announced the first alpha (v0.01) on October 29, 1998 as kernel patches for development kernels 2.1.123 and 2.1.125, supporting only the DoubleTalk synthesizer. Andy Berdan, John Covici, and Brian and David Borowski contributed. Its 2004 FAQ says it works with kernels 2.6.18 or later; Speakup 2.0 covered 2.4 and early 2.6 kernels.
