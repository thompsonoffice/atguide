Detailed Description:
EchoVision, by AGIGA, looks like everyday round-framed glasses in matte black. It measures 135 mm hinge to hinge, with lenses 52 mm wide and 39 mm tall, a 19 mm bridge and 138 mm temples. AGIGA calls it ultra-light but lists no weight.
It has a 13-megapixel camera for photos and video, a four-microphone array, and open-ear directional stereo speakers that sit near your ears without covering them.
There are two physical buttons, one on each side, and a touchpad on the left arm. It also answers voice commands.
The Pioneer Edition runs about 6 hours per charge and the V1 about 9, with up to 30 hours using the charging case. It connects by Bluetooth 5.3 and dual-band Wi-Fi 5 to a companion app for iPhone or Android.
The box includes a charging case, clip-on sunglasses and a getting-started guide in large print and braille. The price is $599.
