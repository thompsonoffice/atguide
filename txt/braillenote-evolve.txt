Detailed Description:
The BrailleNote Evolve is HumanWare's 2026 braille computer, running KeySoft on Windows 11. HumanWare's specification sheet gives 243 mm wide, 175 mm deep and 20 mm thick (9.57 × 6.89 × 0.79 in), or 25 mm (1 in) on its feet, and 990 g (2.18 lb), or about 1.4 kg (3 lb) in its case.
It comes in 20- and 32-cell braille configurations. HumanWare's labeled drawings show the top surface with the braille keyboard and display; the full key layout is described in that sheet's figures, which are image-only.
On the left side edge are ports and buttons: a 3.5 mm audio jack that works with a microphone, an HDMI 1.4 port and two USB-C Thunderbolt 4 ports, both of which can charge it. There is also a USB 3.2 Type-A port.
Inside are an Intel Core Ultra 5 125U processor, 32 GB of LPDDR5x memory and a 512 GB NVMe drive. It has vibration haptics, stereo speakers, Wi-Fi 6, Bluetooth 5 and a programmable RFID tag for future use.
In the box are the Evolve, a carrying case, a nylon strap with shoulder pad, headphones, a power adapter, a print and braille Getting Started guide and command summary, and a modifier sticker.
