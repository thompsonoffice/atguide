Detailed Description:
The explorē 8 is an 8-inch handheld video magnifier from HumanWare. It is 8 inches wide, 6 inches tall and just under an inch thick, and weighs 580 grams, about 1.3 pounds. It has a rubberized grip.
The front is almost entirely an 8-inch touchscreen. Below it is a control panel with three large physical buttons: Zoom minus on the left, a contrast or color button in the middle, and Zoom plus on the right. The zoom buttons change magnification in half steps, or continuously when held.
The left side has, from the top, the power button, an HDMI port for a TV and a USB-C port for charging and a computer. On the top edge, at the right, is a capture button: a press freezes the image, and a long press saves it.
On the back, at the top center, are two cameras, one for close-up reading and one for distance, flanked by two rows of three LED lights.
Along the bottom of the back is a solid alloy folding stand the full width of the device. Opened, it tilts the screen about 30 degrees toward you on a table and switches to the close-up camera.

Its two 21-megapixel cameras cover close work from 8.5 to 15 cm and distance from 15 cm to infinity. It offers 17 color schemes and auto-shutoff after 1, 2, 5 or 10 minutes. Its LED lights turn off when it is lifted. It is for low vision, with no speech output.
