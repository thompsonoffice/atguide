History:
Basic-D V4 (2011) was Index's first black-and-white Basic: a white case with a black lid. The control panel changed to a Menu button with up and down arrows. Production ran from 2011 to 2016. The BrailleBox, a sheet-fed production embosser with three embossing heads and a 300-page sheet feeder, came out in 2011 as part of V4. Index calls it the most advanced development work it had ever done.
Predecessors: Index V3 platform (Index Braille, 2002 to 2011)
Successors: Index V5 line (Basic-D, Everest-D, BrailleBox) (Index Braille, 2016 to present)
