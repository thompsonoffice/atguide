Detailed Description:
The V4 platform includes the Basic-D (tractor-fed), the Everest-D (cut sheets) and the BrailleBox, a production embosser with three embossing heads. According to the entry's history, the Basic-D V4 has a white case with a black lid. The Basic-D and Everest-D run from an external 48-volt switched-mode power supply.
On the Basic-D, the control panel and lid are on top, with left and right tractors underneath. Optical sensors detect the paper edge and paper jams. A transport lock is stored in holders on the back plate. The Everest-D has a top sheet feeder with a lock, left and right margin guides with locking levers, card/sheet selectors and pick-up rollers, plus an adjustable front bottom profile and guides that set the angle of the ejected paper.
The control panel has On, Off, Feed, Help and Menu keys, up, down and left arrows, OK, and number keys 1 and 10, plus a CHS key that switches between increasing and decreasing a value. Spoken feedback, in a local language or English, announces each function. Three LEDs show green for power OK, yellow for online and red for menu active.
On the back of the Basic-D and Everest-D are the 48-volt power input and a reset button (hold 3 seconds for factory settings). There are also a service serial port, a serial port, a USB 2.0 port, a 100-megabit network port with an LED, and a headphone jack. Status LEDs between the USB and service ports show paper-edge and paper-movement sensing.
The BrailleBox takes 90 to 250 volts directly and adds a paper tray, a braille output tray, a service door and LED indicators. It embosses A3, tabloid and similar sheets in newspaper format. The Basic-D takes fanfold paper 1 to 17 inches long and 4 to 13 inches wide, and the Everest-D takes cut sheets from 120 to 590 mm long. Index gives a 2-year parts warranty.

History:
Basic-D V4 (2011) was Index's first black-and-white Basic: a white case with a black lid. The control panel changed to a Menu button with up and down arrows. Production ran from 2011 to 2016. The BrailleBox, a sheet-fed production embosser with three embossing heads and a 300-page sheet feeder, came out in 2011 as part of V4. Index calls it the most advanced development work it had ever done.
Predecessors: Index V3 platform (Index Braille, 2002 to 2011)
Successors: Index V5 line (Basic-D, Everest-D, BrailleBox) (Index Braille, 2016 to present)
