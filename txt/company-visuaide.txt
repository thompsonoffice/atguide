VisuAide
Type: Company profile
Founded: 1988 (Quebec)

History:
A Canadian maker of the Victor Reader DAISY players (Victor Reader Classic, about 2002) and the Trekker GPS (2003). Merged with Pulse Data in January 2005 to form HumanWare.
