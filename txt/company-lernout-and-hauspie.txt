Lernout & Hauspie
Type: Company profile
Founded: Not documented
Products listed: 2

History:
Belgian speech-technology company whose TruVoice and TTS3000 engines were the default speech output for Microsoft Agent characters in the SAPI 4 era.

Products:
L&H TruVoice voices (Adult Male 1 to 8, Adult Female 1 and 2) (TTS Voices, 1998 (SAPI 4 era))
L&H TTS3000 voices (Michelle, Michael and the Centigram-style US voices) (TTS Voices, Late 1990s (L&H versions 6.03 to 6.13))
