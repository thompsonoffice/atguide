History:
Released in 1992, the Everest-D V1 took cut-sheet paper and, Index says, was the first small double-sided braille printer. V1 machines are end-of-life with no parts available.
Predecessors: Index Blue Bar (Index Braille, 1988)
Successors: Index Basic and Everest (V2 platform) (Index Braille, February 1995 to 2002)
