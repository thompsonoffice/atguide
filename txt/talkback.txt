History:
Announced on the Google Open Source Blog on October 20, 2009, with SoundBack and KickBack, when Android 1.6 Donut added an accessibility API. Written by T. V. Raman, Charles Chen and Svetoslav Ganov; early users navigated with the trackball and might need to install it from Android Market. Single-finger gestures came in Android 4.1. Version 6.2 (June 2018) became the Android Accessibility Suite. A braille keyboard came in 8.2 (2020), multi-finger gestures and a new menu in 9.1 (2021), and image captions in 12.1. Since Android 11, Samsung ships its own TalkBack build. Version 17.1 (September 17, 2026) needs Android 9 or later. Open source under Apache 2.0.
