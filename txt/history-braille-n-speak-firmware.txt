Braille 'n Speak firmware
Company: Blazie Engineering
Category: Operating system or software version history
Versions listed: 2

Overview:
The built-in software of Blazie's notetakers. Version-by-version history was not found in the sources researched.

Version History:

Version 1: Original
Released: 1987
Notes: Notetaking, calculator and clock in 200K nonvolatile memory.

Version 2: Braille 'n Speak 2000
Released: 1990s
Notes: Ran from 2 MB flash with 768 KB RAM.

Devices and Software That Ran It:
Braille 'n Speak (original) (Blazie Engineering, 1987 to 1990s)
Braille 'n Speak Classic (Blazie Engineering, Name later given to the original line)
Braille 'n Speak 2000 (Blazie Engineering, 1999 (APH catalog entry))
Type 'n Speak (Blazie Engineering, Late 1992)
Braille Lite 18 (Blazie Engineering, Summer 1993)
Braille Lite 40 (Blazie Engineering, Summer 1996)
Braille Lite M20 / M40 (Freedom Scientific, 2002)
