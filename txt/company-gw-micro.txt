GW Micro
Type: Company profile
Founded: February 15, 1990
Products listed: 2

History:
Founded in Fort Wayne, Indiana by Doug Geoffray and Dan Weirich. Products: the Vocal-Eyes DOS screen reader (1990), the Sounding Board speech card and Window-Eyes (1.0 in October 1995). It was the US distributor of HIMS BrailleSense products from about 2006 to late 2010. Merged with Ai Squared in May 2014. Window-Eyes development ended in 2017 under VFO.
