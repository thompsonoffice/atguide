Detailed Description:
The BrailleSense U2 Mini (2013) fits in a purse, backpack or roomy jacket pocket. It measures 6.7 × 3.5 × 1 inches (172 × 90 × 27 mm) and weighs under a pound (about 427 g).
On top is a nine-key Perkins-style keyboard with four function keys.
It has 18 eight-dot braille cells with 18 routing buttons, and four scroll keys.
A panel of media controls is on the front. The power socket is on the left side, marked with a braille 'dc'. There are an SD card slot, an OTG USB port, a stereo headphone jack and built-in speakers that AccessWorld found clear and loud for the size.
The battery compartment is at the back left edge. Two user-replaceable lithium batteries come with it, with a separate charger, so one can charge while the other is in use; each lasted about 8 hours in AccessWorld's test. It has a vibration motor for silent alerts.
