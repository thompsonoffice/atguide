VoiceView (Amazon Fire tablets)
Company: Amazon
Category: Screen Readers
Released: September 2015 (Fire OS 5 'Bellini')

Overview:
Amazon's own screen reader for Fire tablets, written from scratch to replace the Android TalkBack integration that earlier Fire tablets used.

Specs:
Developer: Amazon
Release years: September 2015 (Fire OS 5 'Bellini')
Runs on: Fire OS 5 and later (Fire tablets)
Default speech: IVONA voices (Amazon)
Price: Free, built into Fire tablets

Physical Description:
Not applicable (software)

Features:
Not documented

History:
Amazon said blind customers had called its TalkBack integration among the best on a tablet, but with a long list of requested fixes. Amazon chose a complete rewrite, and VoiceView shipped with Fire OS 5 'Bellini' in September 2015 using IVONA voices. Its features included linear and touch navigation, a wide range of speech rates, new earcons, simplified gestures, a jog-wheel gesture and a built-in tutorial. By 2017 tens of thousands of customers used it, and braille input and output (BAUM, HumanWare, Orbit Reader 20) came to 4th to 7th generation tablets that September.
