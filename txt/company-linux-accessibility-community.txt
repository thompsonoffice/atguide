Linux accessibility community
Type: Company profile
Founded: Not a company; projects date from 1995 (BRLTTY) and 1998 (Speakup)
Products listed: 10

History:
Independent open-source projects: Speakup (Kirk Reiser, 1998), BRLTTY (Nikhil Nair and James Bowden, 1995), Emacspeak (T. V. Raman, 1994), Fenrir (2016–2017) and the Jenux distribution (Daniel Nash).

Products:
Emacspeak (Screen Readers, October 1994 (open source April 1995) to present)
BRLTTY (Screen Readers, 1995 (0.22 beta) to present)
Speakup (Screen Readers, October 29, 1998 to present)
Oralux (Accessible Linux, July 3, 2003 (0.03))
ADRIANE (Knoppix) (Accessible Linux, 2007 (presented June 2007; part of KNOPPIX since 5.3))
Vinux (Accessible Linux, 2008 to January 18, 2017 (discontinued))
Talking Arch (Accessible Linux, By fall 2009 (Chris Brannon's April 2010 announcement refers to a release in fall 2009); last release October 3, 2017)
Sonar GNU/Linux (Accessible Linux, April 2013 (Sonar 13.04) to 2017)
Fenrir (Screen Readers, Repository July 2016; 1.0 released February 7, 2017)
Jenux (Accessible Linux, Active by January 2023 (the Jenux-2023.01.17 dual ISO on the Blinux list); ISO, Docker and Crostini builds were updated in October 2026.)
