Flite
Company: Carnegie Mellon University
Category: TTS Voices
Released: Version 1.0 beta, August 27, 2001

Overview:
Festival-lite, a small-footprint synthesizer for handhelds and servers.

Specs:
Maker: Carnegie Mellon University
First released: Version 1.0 beta, August 27, 2001
Synthesis type: Concatenative (diphone and unit selection)
Platforms: Embedded, Linux and others
Default on or shipped with: kal, an 8 kHz US English diphone voice derived from cmuuskal and the single voice Flite originally shipped with (later releases also add the awb, rms and slt voices)
Price: Not documented

Not applicable (software)

History:
Alan W Black and colleagues wrote it in 2001 as a reimplementation of Festival's core design. Flite ('festival-lite') was written at Carnegie Mellon University by Alan W. Black and Kevin A. Lenzo as a small, fast run-time engine for voices built with the Festival and FestVox tools, for embedded systems, handhelds and busy servers. They presented it at the 4th ISCA Workshop on Speech Synthesis in 2001, and version 1.0 beta was posted on August 27, 2001, including an ARM Linux binary.
Predecessors: Festival (University of Edinburgh CSTR, 1996 to present)
