NuEyes
Type: Company profile
Founded: January 2016

History:
You wrote this as New Eyes; the company is NuEyes. It makes head-worn low-vision magnifiers: the NuEyes Easy (2016, $5,995) and the Pro, Pro 3 and Pro 4 (up to 12x). It won a CES 2016 award. A veteran-owned company in Orange County, California.

Products:
NuEyes Easy (Vision Wearables, 2016)
NuEyes Pro 3 (Vision Wearables, Announced December 15, 2020)
NuEyes Pro (Pro, Pro 3, Pro 4) (Vision Wearables, By October 2024, when the Pro 4 LV brochure was published. Its predecessor, the Pro 3, was announced on December 15, 2020.)
