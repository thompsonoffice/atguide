L&H TruVoice voices (Adult Male 1 to 8, Adult Female 1 and 2)
Company: Lernout & Hauspie
Category: TTS Voices
Released: 1998 (SAPI 4 era)

Overview:
Lernout & Hauspie's American English TruVoice engine. It was the default speech output for every Microsoft Agent character, such as the Office-era animated characters, and was bundled with Microsoft's Speech API 4 releases.

History:
Microsoft's Agent documentation lists ten TruVoice voices: Adult Male 1 to 8 and Adult Female 1 and 2. All Agent characters were tuned for TruVoice, and other engines needed the SAPI 4.0a runtime. The tvenua.exe package also included novelty voices: RoboSoft One to Six, Female Whisper and Male Whisper. L&H's separate TTS3000 engine supplied other languages, such as Carol and Peter (British English), Anna and Stefan (German), and Véronique and Pierre (French). Microsoft Agent was deprecated with Windows 7. The user's question about a 'Mark Lund' voice could not be verified in any source found.
