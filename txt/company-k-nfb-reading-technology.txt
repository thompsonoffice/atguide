K-NFB Reading Technology
Type: Company profile
Founded: 2005
Products listed: 2

History:
K-NFB Reading Technology was the partnership of inventor Ray Kurzweil and the National Federation of the Blind. Work began around 2002, and the handheld K-NFB Reader went on sale on July 1, 2006. The software was later ported to the Nokia N82 phone as KNFB Reader Mobile ($1,595). In 2014 an iOS app ($99), developed by the NFB and Sensotec NV, followed, and an Android version came shortly after.
