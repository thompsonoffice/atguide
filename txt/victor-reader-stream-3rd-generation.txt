Detailed Description:
The third-generation Victor Reader Stream (2023) measures 114 × 62 × 18 mm (4.5 × 2.4 × 0.7 in) and weighs 110 g, with a fully tactile interface.
Across the top of the front are the Go To, Online (Wi-Fi) and Bookmark keys.
Below them is the 12-key number pad, then Sleep, Rewind, Play/Stop and Fast Forward.
The left side has Power/Toggle, an LED, the Up and Down keys and the microphone jack. The right side has a red Record key.
The SD slot is on the top edge. The bottom edge has a USB-C port, the headphone jack and the speaker. It reads text with two Acapela voices.
