History:
A preview called 'Spoken Interface Preview' appeared for Mac OS X 10.3 Panther. VoiceOver shipped in 10.4 Tiger in 2005, and the Alex voice arrived with 10.5 Leopard in 2007. Mac OS X 10.7 Lion added downloadable Nuance RealSpeak voices.
Predecessors: outSPOKEN for Macintosh (Berkeley Systems, 1989)
