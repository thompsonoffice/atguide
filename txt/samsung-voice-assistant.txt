Samsung Voice Assistant
Company: Samsung
Category: Screen Readers
Released: 2015 (Galaxy S6 / Note 5, Android 5.1.1) until One UI 3.1

Overview:
Samsung's own Galaxy screen reader. It supported multi-finger gestures well before Google's TalkBack did.

History:
It coexisted with TalkBack for years. Samsung discontinued it with One UI 3.0 (Android 11), when an agreement made Samsung's TalkBack build the only built-in reader. That build launched with bugs and initially lacked a braille keyboard.
Successors: TalkBack (Google, October 2009 (Android 1.6 Donut) to present)
