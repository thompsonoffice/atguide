VoiceOver (iOS / iPadOS)
Company: Apple
Category: Screen Readers
Released: iPhone 3GS (2009) to present

Overview:
The gesture-based touchscreen screen reader that made the iPhone accessible to blind users.

History:
Arrived with the iPhone 3GS in 2009, then the 3rd-generation iPod touch, and the iPad from its launch. Later added braille display support (iOS 4, 2010), Braille Screen Input (iOS 8, 2014) and Braille Access (2025).
